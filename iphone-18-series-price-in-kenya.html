Which iPhone 18 Should You Buy in Kenya?

iPhone 18 Pro — Best for Camera & Performance in a Compact Body

The iPhone 18 Pro is the entry point to the series, starting from KSh 187,000. It has the A20 Pro chip (2nm), a 48MP Fusion Main camera with variable aperture (f/1.5–4.0), a 48MP ultrawide and a 48MP 4x periscope telephoto, all in a compact 6.3-inch body that weighs 211g.

Good for: photography and videography, gaming, and buyers who want full Pro performance in a smaller, lighter phone.
Consider upgrading if: you want the largest screen and the longest battery life. That's the Pro Max.

iPhone 18 Pro Max — Best for Battery, Screen & Camera Together

The iPhone 18 Pro Max, from KSh 207,000, pairs the same triple 48MP camera system as the Pro with a 6.9-inch display and the longest battery life ever in an iPhone: up to 45 hours of video playback and up to 30 hours of typical use on eSIM-only models.

Good for: buyers who want everything at once, with the biggest conventional screen, the longest battery and the full Pro camera system.
Consider a lower tier if: budget or weight matters. The Pro Max is 249g, and the iPhone 18 Pro offers the same chip and camera system in a smaller, lighter body.

iPhone Duo — Best for Apple's First Foldable

The iPhone Duo, from KSh 396,000, is Apple's first foldable iPhone. It opens from a 5.4-inch cover display to a 7.6-inch inner display and is just 5.2mm thin when unfolded. It runs the same A20 Pro chip, adds Apple Pencil support, and keeps IP68 water resistance.

Good for: early adopters, multitaskers, note-takers who want Apple Pencil on an iPhone, and anyone who wants a large unfolded screen that folds down to pocket size.
Consider a Pro model if: you want a telephoto zoom lens or Face ID. The Duo has a dual camera (wide + ultrawide) with no telephoto, and it uses Touch ID on the side button instead of Face ID.

Upgrading from an Older iPhone? What's Actually New in the iPhone 18 Series

If you're coming from an iPhone 17, 16 or 15 series device and wondering whether the iPhone 18 is worth it, here's what has changed:

  • A20 Pro chip (2nm) replaces the A19 Pro (3nm). Apple says the GPU is up to 40% faster, the Neural Engine doubles AI processing power, and a new vapor chamber gives roughly 3x the cooling.
  • Variable aperture main camera. The 48MP Fusion Main lens physically adjusts between f/1.5 and f/4.0, and the Camera app now has manual shutter speed, aperture, white balance and exposure controls.
  • Bigger battery gains on the Pro Max. It reaches up to 45 hours of video playback, up from 39 hours on the iPhone 17 Pro Max, and it is 16g heavier as a result.
  • Up to 2TB of storage, the first time an iPhone has offered it.
  • Smaller Dynamic Island, a new Burgundy colour, and the new Apple C2 modem with N1 wireless chip.
  • Apple's first foldable. The iPhone Duo is a new form factor, with nothing like it in the iPhone 17 series.

If you own an iPhone 17 Pro or Pro Max, the iPhone 18 is a solid upgrade, mainly for the faster chip, variable aperture camera and (on the Pro Max) the battery. If you're coming from an iPhone 15 or 16 series device, the jump will be far more noticeable. The iPhone 17 series is still available at a lower price if you'd rather save.

iPhone 18 Pro Max vs Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra: How It Compares

A common question from Kenyan buyers choosing between ecosystems: the Galaxy S26 Ultra's biggest advantages over the iPhone 18 Pro Max are its higher-resolution 200MP main camera, a dedicated S Pen, and Samsung's Privacy Display feature. The iPhone 18 Pro Max holds an edge in video recording consistency, battery endurance (up to 45 hours of video playback) and Apple's longer track record of software support and resale value in the Kenyan market. If you're staying within Android and want the highest-resolution main camera available, the S26 Ultra is the stronger choice; if you're already inside Apple's ecosystem (Mac, iPad, AirPods), switching costs usually outweigh the camera resolution difference.

Specs & Features

What are the main differences between the iPhone 18 Pro, Pro Max and iPhone Duo?

All three use the Apple A20 Pro chip (2nm) and 12GB of RAM. The iPhone 18 Pro has a 6.3-inch display and weighs 211g. The Pro Max has a 6.9-inch display, up to 45 hours of video playback and weighs 249g. The iPhone Duo is a foldable with a 7.6-inch inner display and a 5.4-inch cover display. Which chip does the iPhone 18 series use?

All three models use the Apple A20 Pro, built on a 2-nanometer process with a 6-core CPU and a 7-core GPU. Apple says the GPU is up to 40% faster than the A19 Pro, and the Neural Engine doubles AI processing power.

How long does the iPhone 18 battery last?

Which iPhone 18 model has the best camera?

What is variable aperture on the iPhone 18?

It is a physically adjustable aperture on the 48MP Fusion Main camera. The lens widens for low light and narrows for deeper focus, instead of relying on software alone. The Camera app also gets manual controls for shutter speed, aperture, white balance and exposure.

Does the iPhone Duo have Face ID?

No. The iPhone Duo uses Touch ID built into the side button instead of Face ID. The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max both use Face ID.

Does the iPhone Duo have a telephoto camera?

No. The iPhone Duo has a dual camera system with a 48MP wide lens and a 48MP ultrawide lens. For optical zoom, choose the iPhone 18 Pro or Pro Max.

Does the iPhone Duo support Apple Pencil?

Yes. The iPhone Duo supports Apple Pencil, a first for the iPhone.

What colours does the iPhone 18 come in?

The iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max come in Black, Silver, Glacier and Burgundy. The iPhone Duo comes in Night Sky and Star White.

Do the iPhone 18 models support 5G in Kenya?

Yes. The iPhone 18 Pro, Pro Max and iPhone Duo all support 5G and 4G LTE with Apple's C2 modem, and work on Safaricom, Airtel Kenya and Telkom Kenya. Actual 5G speeds depend on your network's coverage in your area.

Is the iPhone 18 Pro Max worth upgrading to from the iPhone 17 Pro Max?

The main gains are the faster A20 Pro chip, the variable aperture camera and a longer battery rating (up to 45 hours of video playback versus 39 hours on the 17 Pro Max). The 18 Pro Max is also 16g heavier. If you're coming from an iPhone 15 or 16 series, the jump will be much more noticeable.

eSIM & SIM Options

Does the iPhone 18 Pro have a physical SIM slot?

It depends on the model you choose. The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max come as eSIM-only (no SIM tray) or as Sim Card + eSIM. The eSIM-only models get about two hours more video playback because the space is used for battery.

Does the iPhone Duo support a physical SIM card?

No. The iPhone Duo is eSIM-only. Confirm your network supports eSIM activation before ordering if you're switching from a physical SIM.

Should I buy the eSIM-only or the Sim Card + eSIM model?

Choose eSIM-only if your network supports eSIM and you want the lowest price and the longest battery life. Choose Sim Card + eSIM if you swap physical SIMs often or travel.
