Infinix Products Price in Kenya

Infinix is one of the most popular smartphone brands in Kenya, known for strong battery life and genuine value for money — but the brand’s range at Phones Store Kenya goes well beyond phones, covering smartwatches, tablets, earbuds, and accessories too.

Infinix Smartphone Series

  • Infinix Note series (Note 60 Pro, Note Edge): built for multitasking and heavy daily use, with large displays and strong batteries
  • Infinix Hot series (Hot 70 Pro 5G, Hot 70 4G, Hot 60 Pro/Pro Plus/i): Infinix’s most affordable everyday phones, focused on browsing, social media, and reliable battery life
  • Infinix GT series (GT 50 Pro, GT 30 Pro): gaming-focused phones with faster processors and performance-oriented design
  • Infinix Smart series (Smart 20, Smart 9HD): entry-level phones for essential daily use

Infinix Wearables

  • Infinix GT Watch 5 Pro & XWatch N5 Pro: Infinix’s higher-tier smartwatches with fuller feature sets
  • Infinix XWatch N4, H5 Pro, H4 & XWatch 3 WE: a range of affordable everyday smartwatches
  • Infinix XBand XW4B: a simpler fitness band for step and activity tracking

Infinix Tablets, Audio & Accessories

  • Infinix XPad 30E: an affordable tablet for browsing, streaming, and everyday use
  • Infinix XE37 AI Buds & Xbuds 3: wireless earbuds, including a translation-capable ANC model in the XE37
  • Infinix XPower 10 Pro Power Bank: portable charging for on-the-go use
  • Infinix 20W Magnetic Wireless Car Charger: hands-free magnetic charging for compatible phones while driving

Which Infinix Should You Buy?

Want the longest battery life and biggest display for everyday multitasking? Go Note series. On a tight budget but still want a dependable daily phone? The Hot series is Infinix’s real value play. Care about gaming performance? The GT series is built for that specifically. Just need the basics covered affordably? The Smart series does that.

Frequently Asked Questions About Infinix in Kenya

What’s the difference between Infinix Note and Hot series?
Note phones prioritize larger displays, more RAM, and heavier-use performance, while Hot phones are built to be Infinix’s most affordable everyday option.

Is Infinix GT series good for gaming?
Yes — the GT series is specifically tuned for gaming performance, with faster processors and cooling designed for sustained use compared to the standard Note or Hot lines.

Do Infinix XWatch models work with iPhone?
Most Infinix wearables are designed primarily for Android pairing — check the specific model’s compatibility before buying if you use an iPhone.
