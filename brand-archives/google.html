Google’s hardware lineup pairs clean Android software with genuinely useful AI features — and Phones Store Kenya stocks one of the widest Google ranges in Nairobi, spanning multiple Pixel generations, every Pixel Watch model, Fitbit wearables, streaming devices, and more.

Google Pixel Phones in Kenya

We stock Pixel across a huge range of generations, so there’s a real entry point at almost every budget:

  • Pixel 11 series (11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL): the current flagship generation with Google’s latest Tensor chip and AI camera features
  • Pixel 10 series (10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL, 10a, 10 Pro Fold): last generation’s flagship and mid-range, still excellent value
  • Pixel 9 series (9, 9 Pro, 9 Pro XL, 9a): a strong step down in price with most of the same camera intelligence
  • Pixel 8 series (8, 8 Pro, 8a): a genuinely budget-friendly way into recent Pixel software and camera quality
  • Pixel 7, 6, and earlier generations: the most affordable route into Pixel, including select Ex-UK units
  • Pixel Fold: Google’s foldable, for buyers who want tablet-sized screen real estate that folds into a phone

Google Pixel Watch in Kenya

All four generations — Pixel Watch 1 through 4 — combine Wear OS smart features with Fitbit’s health and fitness tracking, including heart rate monitoring and sleep insights synced directly to your Pixel or Android phone.

Fitbit & Wearables

Beyond the Pixel Watch, we also stock standalone Fitbit devices like the Fitbit Air, for buyers who want dedicated fitness tracking without a full smartwatch.

Streaming & Smart Devices

The Google TV Streamer 4K brings smart TV functionality, voice search, and access to every major streaming app to any HDMI television — Google’s modern successor to the original Chromecast line.

Beyond What’s Currently in Stock

Google’s broader hardware ecosystem also includes Pixel Buds (A-series and Pro) for wireless audio, the Pixel Tablet for a larger-screen Android experience, and Nest smart home devices like speakers, displays, and thermostats. Our range is expanding — ask our team if you’re after a specific Google product beyond what’s listed above.

Which Pixel Should You Buy?

Want the best camera and AI features Google currently offers? Go Pixel 11 Pro or 11 Pro XL. Want flagship-level performance without flagship pricing? The Pixel 9 or 10 series hits that balance well. On a tight budget but still want a clean, fast Pixel experience? The a-series (8a, 9a, 10a) consistently punches above its price. Want a genuinely different form factor? The Pixel Fold is Google’s answer to a phone-tablet hybrid.

Frequently Asked Questions About Google Products in Kenya

How long does Google support Pixel phones with software updates?
Recent Pixel generations (8 series onward) receive extended years of OS and security updates, making them a strong long-term choice compared to many Android competitors.

Is the Pixel Watch as good as an Apple Watch or Samsung Galaxy Watch?
For Android and especially Pixel phone owners, the Pixel Watch offers the deepest software integration, plus genuine Fitbit health tracking — though it only pairs with Android, not iPhone.

What’s the difference between the standard Pixel and the “a” series?
The “a” series (like the 9a or 10a) trims some premium hardware — build materials, charging speed, camera hardware — to hit a lower price while keeping the core Pixel software and camera processing intelligence.

Does the Google TV Streamer work with local Kenyan streaming apps?
Yes — alongside Google TV’s built-in content hub, you can install apps like YouTube, Netflix, and Showmax directly.
