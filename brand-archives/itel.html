Itel Phones Price in Kenya

Itel is one of the most popular budget phone brands in Kenya, and Phones Store Kenya stocks a genuinely wide range — from basic KSh 1,450 keypad phones to fuller-featured smartphones across several series.

Itel A Series

Itel’s most affordable smartphone line, covering essentials like calls, messaging, browsing, and light app use. Current models include the A04, A06, A18s, A20, A48, A50, A70, A80, A95, and A200 — spanning genuinely entry-level to slightly more capable configurations.

Itel P Series

The P series focuses on high-capacity batteries for extended daily use, currently including the P55 and P55 Plus, and P65.

Itel S Series

A step up in design and camera performance, with the S24 and S25 Ultra currently in the range.

Itel City & Other Series

  • Itel City 100 & City 200: everyday phones balancing features and affordability
  • Itel Zeno 10 & Zeno 20: part of Itel’s broader everyday smartphone lineup
  • Itel Power 70: built around battery endurance
  • Itel Vision 11: a display-focused everyday option
  • Itel RS4: a further model in the current range
  • Itel 2160: a genuinely basic keypad feature phone at the most accessible price point in the entire catalog — ideal for calls and texts only

Which Itel Should You Buy?

Just need calls and texts at the lowest possible cost? The Itel 2160 keypad phone covers that. Want your first smartphone or a reliable backup device? The A series is Itel’s real entry point. Care most about battery life lasting all day? Go P series. Want a nicer design and better camera within Itel’s budget positioning? The S series is the step up.

Frequently Asked Questions About Itel in Kenya

What’s the difference between Itel A series and P series?
The A series is Itel’s core entry-level smartphone line, while the P series specifically emphasizes larger battery capacity for buyers who prioritize all-day (or multi-day) usage over other features.

Does Itel still make basic keypad phones?
Yes — models like the Itel 2160 remain available for buyers who want a simple, low-cost phone for calls and texts without smartphone features.

Is Itel a reliable brand for a first smartphone?
Itel has built a strong presence in Kenya’s budget segment specifically by focusing on affordability and essential features, making it a common starting point for first-time smartphone buyers.
