Kid-Proof Design

The tablet is built for small hands, drops, and busy days.

Kid-Proof Case with Built-In Stand

The tablet is wrapped in a thick, soft Kid-Proof Case that protects it from bumps and drops and has a built-in stand for hands-free watching and reading. The tablet itself is made of matte plastic that contains 35% post-consumer recycled content.

Colours

Display and Performance

7-Inch IPS Display

The 7-inch IPS touchscreen has a 1024 x 600 resolution (171 ppi), good for cartoons, videos, e-books, and learning apps.

Quad-Core Processor and 2GB RAM

A 2GHz quad-core processor and 2GB of RAM keep apps and videos running smoothly. It is about 30% faster than the previous generation and has twice the RAM.

16GB or 32GB Storage

Choose 16GB or 32GB of built-in storage and expand it with a microSD card (sold separately) for up to 1TB more space. It runs Fire OS 8.

Safe and Parent-Friendly

Parental Controls and Ad-Free Content

Parents can set parental controls to manage screen time, content, and apps. The kids’ experience is ad-free, so children see only what you choose to share. The tablet includes a 1-year Amazon Kids+ subscription where available.

Battery, Cameras and Connectivity

10 Hours of Battery Life

The 3,750mAh battery lasts up to 10 hours of reading, video, web browsing, and music. It charges through USB-C in about 4 hours, easy for children to plug in and best done overnight.

Front and Rear Cameras

Two 2MP cameras, front and rear, support 720p HD video for photos and video calls with family.

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Headphone Jack

It has Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, a 3.5mm headphone jack, and a built-in speaker.

What’s in the Box

The package includes the Fire 7 tablet, the Kid-Proof Case, a USB-C cable, a 5W power adapter, and a quick start guide.

Frequently Asked Questions About Amazon Devices in Kenya

Is a Fire tablet as good as an iPad? Not quite in raw performance or app ecosystem — Fire tablets run a customized Android-based OS with access to the Amazon Appstore rather than Google Play — but for streaming, reading, and browsing at a fraction of the price, most everyday users won't notice the difference. Does the Fire TV Stick work with local Kenyan streaming apps? Yes — alongside Amazon Prime Video, you can install apps like YouTube, Netflix, and Showmax directly from the Fire TV app store. What's the real difference between Kindle and reading on a phone or tablet? Kindle's e-ink screen doesn't emit light the way phone and tablet screens do, so there's far less eye strain during long reading sessions, plus battery life lasts weeks instead of hours. Are Fire Kids tablets actually kid-proof? They come with a reinforced protective case and Amazon's Kid-Friendly Guarantee, which covers free replacement if the tablet breaks from normal use within the warranty period — check current terms with our team when purchasing.
