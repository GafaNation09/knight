Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2

The Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2 Price in Kenya is Ksh 8,000. The Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2 combines style, versatility, and functionality, making it a standout in the smartwatch market. 

Interchangeable Bezels and Straps

One of the best design features of the Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2 is its interchangeable bezels and straps, offering a high level of customization. The watch has various bezel options, including Dark Grey Curved, Dark Grey Flat, Ash Grey Curved, and Ash Grey Flat bezels. This allows users to switch between styles depending on their preference or occasion, adding a layer of personalization rarely seen in other smartwatches.

The watch straps are also removable and come in two main material options. The Dark Grey and Ash Grey models feature a liquid silicon strap, providing a comfortable, skin-friendly, and durable choice for everyday wear. On the other hand, the Blue and Orange models come with leather straps, adding a touch of sophistication and style, which is ideal for more formal settings. This versatility in design allows users to tailor their watches to fit both casual and formal environments seamlessly.

Materials and Build Quality

The Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2 boasts a metal body that enhances its durability and gives it a premium feel. The metal ensures longevity and adds a sense of solidity to the watch, making it a reliable accessory for daily use.

Liquid silicon for the straps in the Dark Grey and Ash Grey models ensures a comfortable fit that is resistant to wear and tear. In contrast, the leather straps in the Blue and Orange models provide a luxurious touch, catering to those who prefer a more traditional look.

Size and Weight

The watch is designed to be compact and lightweight, ensuring it is comfortable to wear throughout the day. The Dark Grey and Ash Grey models measure 25.5 x 4.5 x 1.36 cm and weigh 48.1g, while the Blue and Orange models measure the same in dimensions but weigh slightly less at 44.4g. This subtle difference in weight is likely due to the difference in strap materials, with the leather strap being marginally lighter. The watch’s size and weight are well-balanced, ensuring it sits comfortably on the wrist without being too bulky or heavy.

Functional Crown and User Interface

A notable feature of the CMF Watch Pro 2 is a functional crown, which enhances the user experience by intuitively navigating the watch’s interface. The crown is designed to be responsive and easy to use, allowing users to scroll through menus and options effortlessly. This feature adds a tactile element to the watch’s functionality, making it more interactive and user-friendly.

Color Options

The watch is available in various colors, including Dark Grey, Ash Grey, Blue, and Orange, providing options for different tastes and styles. The color options are thoughtfully chosen to cater to a broad audience, from those who prefer understated, classic hues to those who like a pop of color in their accessories.

Display on the Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2

The Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2’s display is one of its most impressive features. It combines advanced technology with a design that prioritizes aesthetics and functionality.

AMOLED Display

At the heart of the CMF Watch Pro 2’s visual experience is a 1.32-inch AMOLED display. AMOLED technology is known for its vibrant colors, deep blacks, and high contrast ratios, making it a perfect choice for a smartwatch where readability and visual appeal are crucial. The colors on this display are rich and saturated, offering a visually engaging experience whether navigating through the watch’s menus, reading notifications, or checking the time. The deep blacks and high contrast provided by the AMOLED panel also enhance the overall clarity of the display, ensuring that all information is easily readable even in dim lighting conditions.

Resolution and Pixel Density

The display boasts a 466 x 466 pixels resolution, translating to a pixel density of 353 PPI (pixels per inch). This high pixel density ensures that text and images appear sharp and detailed, with no visible pixelation. The resolution is impressive for a screen of this size, allowing for a clear and crisp visual experience that is easy on the eyes. Whether you’re reading small text or viewing intricate watch faces, the display offers a level of detail that enhances the overall user experience.

Brightness and Visibility

One of the standout features of the CMF Watch Pro 2’s display is its peak brightness of 620 nits. This brightness level ensures the screen remains highly visible even in bright outdoor environments. The display remains straightforward to read whether you’re checking your watch under direct sunlight or in other challenging lighting conditions. The auto-brightness feature further enhances usability by adjusting the screen brightness based on the ambient light, ensuring that the display is always optimized for the best viewing experience without draining too much battery life.

Refresh Rate

The display also supports a 60 fps refresh rate, which is relatively high for a smartwatch. This higher refresh rate ensures smooth animations and transitions, creating a more fluid and responsive user experience. Whether scrolling through notifications, navigating menus, or interacting with various apps, the 60 fps refresh rate provides a smooth and seamless interface, enhancing the watch’s overall usability.

Fitness and Health Capabilities of the Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2

The Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2 is designed with a comprehensive suite of fitness and health features, making it a powerful tool for those serious about their well-being. 

Advanced Fitness Tracking

120 Sports Modes with Smart Recognition: The CMF Watch Pro 2 has an extensive range of 120 sports modes, covering almost any physical activity—from running and cycling to swimming and more niche sports. This variety ensures that the watch can accurately track your performance, no matter your sport or workout preference. Additionally, it features five innovative recognition activities, which automatically detect and start tracking joint exercises like walking and running, making it easier to stay on top of your workouts without needing to start a session manually.

Training Goals Setting: The watch allows you to set training goals tailored to your needs, helping you stay motivated and reach your fitness targets. Whether you’re aiming for a specific number of steps, calories burned, or exercise duration, the watch keeps you on track by providing real-time feedback and progress updates.

My Heart Rate & Warm-up Exercises: The “My Heart Rate” feature gives you immediate access to your current heart rate, helping you monitor your cardiovascular performance during workouts. This is complemented by warm-up exercise suggestions, ensuring that your body is correctly prepared before engaging in more intense physical activities, reducing the risk of injury.

Comprehensive Health Monitoring

24-Hour Heart Rate and SpO₂ Monitoring: The watch offers continuous 24-hour heart rate monitoring, constantly checking your pulse throughout the day and during exercise. This is particularly useful for identifying trends and ensuring your heart rate remains within healthy limits. In addition, the SpO₂ sensor monitors your blood oxygen levels, providing valuable insights into your overall respiratory health. This can be particularly important for athletes training at high altitudes or users monitoring their general well-being.

Smart Sleep Algorithm: Sleep is a critical component of overall health, and the CMF Watch Pro 2’s Smart Sleep Algorithm provides detailed insights into your sleep patterns. Tracking different sleep stages—light, deep, and REM—gives you a comprehensive overview of your sleep quality. This feature helps you make informed decisions to improve your rest, essential for recovery and overall well-being.

Menstrual Cycle Tracking: The watch includes menstrual cycle tracking for female users, offering a discreet and convenient way to monitor your cycle. This feature provides reminders and predictions for upcoming periods, helping users stay prepared and informed about their reproductive health.

Active Score and All-Day Monitoring: The watch’s Active Score feature gives you a daily assessment of your overall activity level, encouraging you to stay active and meet your fitness goals. In addition to tracking specific workouts, the watch counts your steps and monitors calorie burn throughout the day. This all-day tracking provides a comprehensive view of your daily activity, making it easier to maintain a healthy lifestyle.

Health Alerts: The CMF Watch Pro 2 includes alerts for abnormal heart rates and blood oxygen levels to ensure you are aware of potential health issues. Suppose your heart rate spikes unexpectedly or your blood oxygen drops to a concerning level. In that case, the watch will notify you, allowing you to take appropriate action or seek medical advice if necessary.

Battery Life on the Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2

The battery life of the Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2 is one of its standout features, offering impressive longevity that caters to various usage patterns and lifestyle needs.

The CMF Watch Pro 2 has a 305 mAh battery, which, while modest in size, delivers exceptional performance due to the watch’s optimized power management system. The battery is designed to keep up with different activity levels, from typical day-to-day use to more demanding tasks like continuous GPS tracking and Bluetooth calls.

For the average user, the watch can last up to 11 days on a single charge. This endurance level is perfect for those who engage in regular activities such as daily fitness tracking, receiving notifications, and occasional calls. It means you can go over a week without worrying about charging, making it highly convenient for busy lifestyles.

Even under more demanding conditions, such as frequent GPS use, continuous heart rate monitoring, and active engagement with the watch’s features, the CMF Watch Pro 2 still offers a solid 9 days of battery life. This makes it suitable for users who rely heavily on their smartwatch throughout the day, ensuring they stay connected and informed without constantly reaching for a charger.

For those who prioritize battery life above all else, the watch’s power-saving mode extends its endurance to an incredible 45.8 days. In this mode, essential functions are preserved. At the same time, non-critical features are minimized or disabled, making it ideal for long trips, outdoor adventures, or situations where access to charging might be limited.

The watch supports up to 17.4 hours of continuous Bluetooth calls, ensuring you can stay connected for extended periods without interruption. This feature is handy for professionals who are on calls throughout the day or users who prefer the convenience of hands-free communication.

For outdoor enthusiasts or athletes who rely on GPS tracking for activities like running, cycling, or hiking, the CMF Watch Pro 2 provides up to 25 hours of continuous GPS use. This endurance level ensures you can comprehensively track your routes and performance data without draining the battery too quickly.

The CMF Watch Pro 2 uses a charging line, and it takes about 100 minutes to recharge the battery from 0% to 100% fully. Given the impressive battery life, this charging time is relatively short, allowing you to quickly top up the battery and get back to your activities without much downtime.

Other Features and Connectivity of the Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2

The Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2 is designed to be a versatile and feature-rich smartwatch. It offers various functionalities that cater to everyday use and specific needs. Here’s a detailed review of its positioning, water resistance, connectivity, and intelligent features.

Positioning Capabilities

The CMF Watch Pro 2 has multi-satellite positioning systems, including GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, QZSS, and Beidou. This extensive range of positioning services ensures that you can accurately track your location and activities, whether you’re navigating through an urban environment or exploring the outdoors. The combination of these systems provides reliable and precise location data, making it an excellent companion for runners, hikers, and outdoor enthusiasts.

Water Resistance

With an IP68 water resistance rating, the CMF Watch Pro 2 is built to withstand dust, dirt, and immersion in water up to 1.5 meters for 30 minutes. This makes it suitable for everyday activities such as washing your hands or getting caught in the rain and more intensive activities like swimming. The watch’s durability ensures it can handle the rigors of an active lifestyle without compromising performance.

Built-In Microphone and Speaker

Including an inbuilt microphone and speaker adds significant convenience to the CMF Watch Pro 2. These features enable you to make and receive Bluetooth calls directly from your wrist, eliminating the need to reach your phone. Additionally, the speaker supports functions like voice assistant activation and audio playback, enhancing the watch’s hands-free capabilities.

Connectivity and App Integration

The watch has Bluetooth 5.3, which offers faster connection speeds, improved range, and more stable connections compared to previous versions. This ensures seamless pairing with your smartphone, allowing for smooth synchronization of data and notifications.


The watch is compatible with the CMF Watch App, which is available for both iOS (version 13 and above) and Android (version 8.0 and above) devices. The app serves as the central hub for managing the watch’s settings, tracking your fitness data, and customizing watch faces. It offers a user-friendly interface that makes it easy to personalize your experience and get the most out of the watch’s features.

Smart Features

The CMF Watch Pro 2 is packed with intelligent features that enhance its utility and convenience:

  • Bluetooth Call: Make and receive calls directly from the watch, thanks to the built-in microphone and speaker.
  • Cloud and Photo Album Watch Faces: Customize your watch face with various options, including dynamic cloud-based faces and personal photos.
  • Breathing Training: A feature that guides you through breathing exercises to help manage stress and improve relaxation.
  • Message Notification: Receive notifications for calls, texts, and app alerts, keeping you connected without checking your phone.
  • Music Control: Manage your music playback directly from the watch, allowing you to skip tracks, play, or pause without pulling out your phone.
  • Timers, Stopwatch, and Alarms: Built-in tools to help you manage your time efficiently.
  • Weather and World Clock: Stay informed about current weather conditions and time zones worldwide.
  • Find My Phone and Flashlight: Handy tools to locate your misplaced phone and illuminate your surroundings.
  • Camera Remote: Use the watch as a remote shutter for your smartphone’s camera, making it easier to take group photos or selfies.
  • Voice Assistant: Integrated voice assistant for quick access to information and tasks.
  • Calendar and Calculator: Essential tools for managing your schedule and performing quick calculations.

Outlook

The Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2 is a well-rounded smartwatch that offers a compelling blend of advanced features, robust connectivity, and practical utilities. Its multi-satellite positioning system and water resistance make it suitable for both casual and active users. At the same time, the wide range of intelligent features ensures it can adapt to your daily needs. Whether you’re looking for a reliable fitness tracker, a convenient communication tool, or a versatile accessory, the CMF Watch Pro 2 delivers on all fronts.
