Design and Build Quality

The Tecno Camon 50 Ultra features a premium design that combines elegance with extreme durability. Despite housing a large battery, the phone maintains a modern look with a curved AMOLED display that blends seamlessly into the frame.

Its build quality is one of its biggest highlights. With IP69K water and dust resistance, the device goes beyond standard protection, allowing it to withstand high-pressure water jets and harsh environments. This is further reinforced by MIL-STD-810H certification, meaning the phone is tested for drops, temperature extremes, and rough handling.

The result is a smartphone that feels both premium and tough—ideal for users who want a device that can survive demanding conditions without sacrificing aesthetics.

Display Quality

The Camon 50 Ultra features a 6.78-inch AMOLED display with 1.5K resolution, delivering sharp visuals and vibrant colors. The curved design enhances immersion, making it ideal for watching videos, gaming, and browsing.

One of its standout features is the 144Hz refresh rate, which ensures ultra-smooth scrolling and fluid animations. Whether you are navigating apps or playing games, the experience feels fast and responsive.

Additionally, the display reaches up to 4500 nits peak brightness, making it one of the brightest screens in its category. This ensures excellent visibility even under direct sunlight, a key advantage for outdoor use in Kenya.

Camera Performance

Rear Cameras

The Tecno Camon 50 Ultra is built with photography in mind. Its triple-camera system is led by a 50MP Sony LYT-700C sensor with Optical Image Stabilization, delivering sharp images with improved dynamic range and stability.

The addition of a 50MP telephoto lens with 3x optical zoom allows users to capture distant subjects without losing detail. With AI SuperZoom extending up to 60x, the camera becomes highly versatile for travel, events, and creative photography.

An 8MP ultra-wide camera completes the setup, making it easy to capture landscapes and group shots.

Selfie Camera

On the front, the phone features a 50MP autofocus selfie camera with eye-tracking technology. This ensures subjects remain sharp and in focus, especially when recording videos or taking portraits.

The result is a camera system that performs exceptionally well for social media content, vlogging, and everyday photography.

Performance and Software

Powering the Tecno Camon 50 Ultra is the MediaTek Dimensity 7400 Ultimate chipset, built on a 4nm architecture. This processor delivers efficient performance, balancing power and battery efficiency.

With up to 12GB of RAM and fast storage, the device handles multitasking smoothly, allowing users to switch between apps, stream content, and play games without lag.

In real-world usage, the phone performs well across everyday tasks and moderate gaming. While it may not match flagship gaming phones, it delivers a consistent and reliable experience for most users.

Battery Life and Charging

Battery performance is one of the strongest aspects of the Camon 50 Ultra. The massive 6500mAh battery is designed to last up to two days under typical usage, making it ideal for users who rely heavily on their smartphones.

The 45W fast charging ensures the battery can be quickly replenished when needed. Additionally, bypass charging support helps reduce heat during gaming by powering the device directly instead of charging the battery.

This combination of large capacity and fast charging makes the device highly dependable for travel, work, and entertainment.
