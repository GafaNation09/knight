Porodo FitPulse Pro Smart Watch Price in Kenya

The Porodo FitPulse Pro Smart Watch price in Kenya is KSh 6,800, bringing a vivid 1.43-inch AMOLED display, Bluetooth calling, and up to 7 days of battery life to an everyday smartwatch. Built with a durable aluminium and zinc alloy case, it suits anyone who wants health tracking, notifications, and a stylish look on the wrist.

Display and Design

A sharp screen and a premium build make the FitPulse Pro easy to enjoy at work and at the gym.

1.43-Inch AMOLED Display

The 1.43-inch AMOLED display has a 466 x 466 resolution, giving crisp text, rich colours, and clear readability indoors and in bright sunlight. Raise-to-wake turns the screen on when you lift your wrist.

Aluminium and Zinc Alloy Case

The case combines aluminium and zinc alloy for a premium look with solid durability. The watch weighs about 42g, and a magnetic band makes it simple to put on and take off.

Water Resistance

The watch is water resistant, protecting it against sweat, rain, and everyday splashes during workouts and daily wear.

Health and Fitness Tracking

Heart Rate and Blood Oxygen

Built-in monitoring covers heart rate and blood oxygen (SpO2) so you can keep track of your health through the day.

Sleep Analysis and Sports Modes

Sleep analysis helps you understand your rest, while multiple sports modes track your workouts from walking and running to gym sessions.

Smart Features

Bluetooth Calling and Notifications

Over Bluetooth 5.2, the watch delivers app notifications and supports Bluetooth calling, so you can take calls from your wrist.

Music Control and Voice Assistant

Control the music on your phone with music control and use the voice assistant for hands-free commands.

Customisable Watch Faces

Choose from customisable watch faces to match your outfit and mood.

Android and iOS Compatibility

It works with Android 8.0 and above and iOS 10.0 and above.

Battery Life

Working Time and Charging

A 400mAh battery delivers up to 7 days of working time, and a full charge takes about 2 hours.
