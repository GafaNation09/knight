JBL Tour Pro 2 Price in Kenya

The JBL Tour Pro 2 price in Kenya is KSh 22,500, bringing JBL’s flagship true wireless earbuds with a 1.45-inch touchscreen Smart Charging Case. Combining True Adaptive Noise Cancelling, Pro Sound, and spatial audio with up to 40 hours of battery life, they are built for commuters, travellers, and anyone who wants premium wireless audio with smarter controls.

Smart Charging Case

The case is the feature that sets the Tour Pro 2 apart from other earbuds.

1.45-Inch Touchscreen Case

The Smart Charging Case has a 1.45-inch touchscreen that lets you control earbud settings, manage calls and playback, adjust audio settings, check battery levels, and set an alarm, all without taking out your phone or opening the JBL Headphones app.

Works Beyond Your Phone

All app features can be reached through the case, even from devices that do not run the app, such as laptops, PCs, and TVs.

Sound Quality

JBL Pro Sound with 10mm Drivers

The earbuds use 10mm dynamic drivers tuned for JBL Pro Sound, with a 20Hz to 20kHz frequency response for powerful bass and clear detail.

JBL Spatial Sound

JBL Spatial Sound creates a wider, more immersive soundstage for music, movies, and games.

Noise Control and Calls

True Adaptive Noise Cancelling

True Adaptive Noise Cancelling adjusts in real time to your surroundings, blocking distractions so you can enjoy your audio or just the sound of silence. Smart Ambient lets outside sound in when you need to stay aware.

6-Mic Call Quality

A 6-microphone array keeps your voice clear on calls in busy or windy places. Voice control supports hands-free commands, and Personal Sound Amplification boosts voices around you without removing the earbuds.

Battery Life and Charging

Up to 40 Hours of Playback

The earbuds deliver up to 10 hours of playback with ANC off and up to 8 hours with ANC on. With the charging case, total listening time reaches up to 40 hours.

Wireless and USB-C Charging

The case supports wireless charging and USB-C charging, with a full charge from empty in about 2 hours.

Connectivity and Design

Bluetooth 5.3 and LE Audio

The earbuds connect over Bluetooth 5.3 with LE Audio support, and Google Fast Pair and Microsoft Swift Pair make pairing quick.

Lightweight and Water Resistant

Each earbud weighs about 6.1g and the case weighs 73g. The earbuds are IPX5 water resistant, ready for sweat and light rain.

Frequently Asked Questions About JBL in Kenya

What's the difference between JBL's PartyBox and portable speaker lines? PartyBox speakers are built for high-volume events with light shows and much larger output, while the standard portable line (like the Xtreme 5) is designed for everyday outdoor listening in a smaller, more portable form. Which JBL headphones have noise cancellation? Active noise cancellation is available on the Tour One M3, Tour One M3 Smart TX, Live 780NC, Live 680NC, and Tune 680NC — the more budget-focused Tune 530BT and Quantum 100 don't include it. Are JBL Endurance earbuds actually sweatproof? Yes — the Endurance line is built with sweat and water resistance ratings specifically for workout use, unlike JBL's general-purpose earbuds.
