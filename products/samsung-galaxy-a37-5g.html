Design and Build Quality

The Samsung Galaxy A37 5G features a sleek and modern design that feels premium despite being a mid-range device. With a slim profile measuring just 7.4mm thick and weighing 195g, the phone is comfortable to hold and easy to use for long periods.

Samsung has used Gorilla Glass Victus+ on both the front and back, which significantly improves durability compared to typical plastic builds in this price range. The plastic frame helps keep the device lightweight while maintaining structural strength.

The inclusion of IP67 water and dust resistance adds extra reliability, making the phone suitable for everyday environments where accidental splashes or dust exposure may occur. Color options such as Lavender, Black, White, and Lime give users a chance to choose a style that fits their personality.

Display Quality

The Galaxy A37 5G comes with a 6.7-inch Super AMOLED display, which is one of its biggest highlights. Samsung is known for producing some of the best smartphone displays, and this device continues that tradition.

The 120Hz refresh rate ensures smooth scrolling and fluid animations, making everything from social media browsing to gaming feel more responsive. With a resolution of 1080 x 2340 pixels, the screen delivers sharp visuals with excellent clarity.

Thanks to AMOLED technology, users get deep blacks, vibrant colors, and strong contrast levels, making it ideal for watching videos, viewing photos, and general use. The always-on display feature also adds convenience by showing notifications and time without unlocking the phone.

Camera Performance

Rear Camera

The Samsung Galaxy A37 5G is equipped with a triple camera system designed for everyday photography. The main 50MP sensor captures detailed images with good color accuracy and dynamic range.

Optical Image Stabilization (OIS) helps reduce blur, especially in low-light conditions or when shooting videos. The 8MP ultra-wide camera is useful for landscape shots and group photos, while the 5MP macro lens allows for close-up photography.

Video recording supports 4K at 30fps, delivering stable and clear footage for content creation and social media.

Selfie Camera

On the front, the device features a 12MP selfie camera that produces sharp and natural-looking images. It is well-suited for video calls, selfies, and social media content.

The front camera also supports 4K video recording, making it a solid option for vloggers and creators who rely on front-facing cameras.

Performance and Software

The Galaxy A37 5G delivers reliable performance for everyday tasks such as browsing, streaming, messaging, and multitasking. It is powered by a capable mid-range processor paired with up to 8GB of RAM, ensuring smooth operation across most applications.

Running on Android 15 with One UI 7, the device offers a clean and user-friendly interface. Samsung’s software is known for its customization options and useful features, including multitasking tools and enhanced privacy settings.

While it may not be designed for heavy gaming at the highest settings, it performs well with popular titles and handles multitasking efficiently.

Battery Life and Charging

Battery life is another strong area of the Samsung Galaxy A37 5G. The device is equipped with a 5000mAh battery, which easily lasts a full day of moderate to heavy usage.

Whether you’re streaming videos, browsing the web, or using social media, the battery is built to keep up with your daily routine. The phone also supports 45W fast charging, allowing you to quickly recharge and get back to using your device without long waiting times.

Samsung Galaxy A37 5G vs Samsung Galaxy A35 5G

The Samsung Galaxy A37 5G offers several improvements over the Samsung Galaxy A35 5G, making it a better choice for users who want a more refined experience.

The A37 features a smoother 120Hz display, faster 45W charging, and a more premium build with Gorilla Glass Victus+. On the other hand, the Galaxy A35 5G is more affordable and still delivers solid performance for everyday use.

If you want better durability, faster charging, and a more modern design, the Galaxy A37 5G is the better option.
