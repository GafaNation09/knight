Tecno T101 Dual Sim Price in Kenya, Full Specifications and Review (2026)

The Tecno T101 Dual Sim price in Kenya is KSh 3200, and it occupies the absolute floor of what a working mobile phone costs in Kenya today. This is Tecno’s most basic feature phone, built around a 1.77-inch QVGA display, a 0.8MP rear camera with mobile torch flash, dual SIM support, Bluetooth and FM radio, and a 1000mAh battery, running on the MT6261D processor and Tecno’s Mos operating system.

There’s nothing pretending to be more than it is here, and that’s genuinely the point. At 114.5 x 49 x 14.2mm, this is a slim, pocketable device built to do exactly four things well: make calls, send texts, play FM radio, and provide just enough camera capability for the occasional casual snapshot. The internet browser, music player, and video player included in the software are genuine bonuses at this price rather than headline features, giving buyers a little more utility than a purely voice-and-text device without pretending to compete with even the most basic smartphone.

At KSh 1,499, the Tecno T101 sits below the Tecno T528 Dual SIM Feature Phone at KSh 1,999, also available at Phonesstore Kenya, with the T101’s lower price as its main appeal, while the T528 counters with a larger 3.0-inch display, a QWERTY keypad for faster texting, and a considerably larger 2500mAh battery for buyers willing to spend a little more.

Design and Build Quality

At just 114.5 x 49 x 14.2mm, the T101 is genuinely one of the more compact and slim options in the feature phone category, easily slipping into any pocket without adding noticeable bulk. Tecno leans into this slim profile as a genuine design highlight rather than an afterthought, and for a phone at this price, the attention paid to keeping the body slender and comfortable to hold is a small but appreciated touch.

The single Black colourway keeps things simple, and the overall build reflects exactly what a phone at this price should prioritize, durability and pocketability over aesthetic flourishes, without cutting corners on the basic construction quality needed for daily handling.

Display and Everyday Use

The 1.77-inch QVGA display at 128 x 160 pixels is about as basic as displays get on a functioning mobile phone, built purely for readability of text, contact lists, and simple menu navigation rather than any richer visual experience. This is precisely the trade-off that keeps the T101’s price at the absolute entry level, and buyers going in should understand this display exists to support calling and texting, not media consumption in any meaningful sense.

Camera and Multimedia

The 0.8MP rear camera with a mobile torch flash provides just enough photographic capability for the most casual, low-stakes use, a quick reference photo or a simple keepsake image, rather than anything resembling genuine photography. The mobile torch doubling as camera flash is a practical dual-use feature that adds genuine everyday utility beyond just the camera function itself, functioning as a basic flashlight when needed.

The inclusion of an internet browser, music player, and video player is a genuinely pleasant surprise at this price point, even though functionality will be limited by the phone’s basic hardware and small display. For users without any other internet access, even a rudimentary browser provides a baseline connection to information and services that would otherwise be unavailable.

Connectivity and Storage

Dual SIM support gives users the flexibility to manage two separate lines on one device, a genuinely practical feature in the Kenyan market for separating personal and business contacts or taking advantage of different carrier promotions. Bluetooth connectivity and FM radio round out a connectivity package that, while basic, covers the core communication and entertainment needs of the phone’s target buyer without unnecessary complexity.

The dedicated microSD card slot supporting expansion up to 32GB is a meaningful addition given the phone’s minimal 32MB of built-in storage and RAM, making expandable storage essentially necessary for anyone wanting to store more than the most basic amount of contacts, messages, or media.

Battery Life

The 1000mAh battery is modest even by feature phone standards, though it’s worth noting that on a device with this minimal power draw, small display, basic processor, and no smartphone-grade background processes, even this capacity should translate into a genuinely useful number of days on standby between charges, a core value proposition for buyers prioritizing simplicity and longevity over raw battery capacity numbers.

Who Is the Tecno T101 For?

Ideal for:

  • Buyers on the absolute tightest budget who need a genuinely functional communication device
  • Anyone who wants a basic backup phone for emergencies or travel
  • Users who want simple call, text, and FM radio functionality without any added complexity
  • First-time phone users or elderly users who prefer straightforward, uncomplicated devices

Not ideal for:

  • Anyone expecting meaningful photo or video quality from the camera
  • Users who want a larger display or a QWERTY keypad for faster texting
  • Smartphone users who need apps, social media, or messaging platforms like WhatsApp
  • Buyers who prioritize maximum battery capacity above the lowest possible price

Pros and Cons

The Tecno T101 Dual Sim has honest, budget-focused strengths at KSh 3200. It delivers dual SIM support, a basic 0.8MP camera with mobile torch flash, Bluetooth and FM radio, a built-in internet browser, music and video players, expandable storage up to 32GB, and a slim, compact, pocketable design, at KSh 1,499, one of the most affordable functioning mobile phones available in Kenya.

Its main limitations are the very basic 1.77-inch QVGA display, minimal 32MB of internal storage and RAM, a modest 1000mAh battery, 2G-only network connectivity, and camera quality that produces only the most basic, low-resolution images and video.
