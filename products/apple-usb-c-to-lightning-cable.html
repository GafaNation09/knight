Apple USB-C to Lightning Cable Price in Kenya, Full Specifications and Review (2026)

The Apple USB-C to Lightning Cable price in Kenya is KSh 1,800. It connects a USB-C power adapter or computer to any Apple device with a Lightning port. That covers iPhones from the iPhone 5 through the iPhone 14 series, older iPads, earlier AirPods cases and some Apple accessories. Paired with a suitable USB-C adapter, it enables fast charging on compatible iPhones, and it also syncs and transfers data.

The USB-C plug is the reason to choose this cable over Apple’s older USB-A version. Fast charging on iPhones uses USB Power Delivery, which works only through USB-C. With a USB-C to Lightning cable and an adapter of 20W or more, iPhone 8 and later models can charge from empty to roughly half full in about 30 minutes, much faster than the slow trickle from an old USB-A cube. For the many people in Kenya still using an iPhone 11, 12, 13 or 14, including Ex-UK units, this cable unlocks the fastest charging their phones support.

The USB-C to Lightning cable costs KSh 700 less and charges compatible iPhones much faster, provided the owner has a USB-C adapter.

Fast Charging Explained

Apple’s fast charging for Lightning iPhones relies on USB Power Delivery (PD), a standard in which the charger and phone agree on a higher voltage and current than basic USB charging provides. PD works only over USB-C, which is why the USB-C end of this cable matters. A Lightning to USB-A cable plugged into an old cube charger delivers about 5W, the slow speed many iPhone owners are used to. With this USB-C cable and an adapter rated 20W or higher, compatible iPhones (iPhone 8 and later) can charge to around 50% in about 30 minutes.

Speed tapers as the battery fills. The last 20% charges more slowly to protect battery health, so a full charge takes longer than double the half-charge time. The adapter is sold separately. Buyers with an old USB-A cube will need a USB-C adapter to benefit, such as Apple’s 20W USB-C adapter, which Phonesstore Kenya stocks at KSh 3,500.

Data Transfer and Connectivity

The cable also connects a Lightning device to a computer’s USB-C port for syncing, backups and file transfers. That is useful for moving photos to a MacBook or a Windows laptop, both of which now mostly have USB-C ports. Lightning iPhones transfer data at USB 2 speeds, which is fine for photos and documents but slow for large video libraries. Many cars with USB-C ports also use this cable for wired Apple CarPlay.

Compatibility

The cable works with any Apple device that has a Lightning port, including:

  • iPhones: iPhone 5 through iPhone 14 series, including iPhone SE models
  • iPads: older iPads with Lightning ports, such as the 9th-generation iPad and earlier
  • AirPods: earlier AirPods and AirPods Pro charging cases with Lightning ports
  • Accessories: older Apple accessories that charge via Lightning

It does not work with iPhone 15 and later or USB-C iPads, which use a USB-C to USB-C cable instead. Buyers should check which port their device has before ordering.

Design and Authenticity

The source describes the cable as compact and convenient for home, work and travel. Its length and whether it has a braided jacket are not specified. Apple sells this cable in 1-metre and 2-metre versions, so buyers needing to reach a distant socket should confirm the length.

Lightning cables are among the most counterfeited accessories in Kenya. Fake cables often charge slowly, fail quickly or trigger the “accessory not supported” warning on iPhones. Buying a genuine Apple or certified cable from a reputable retailer avoids these problems.

Apple USB-C to Lightning Cable vs Apple Lightning to USB Cable (1m)
