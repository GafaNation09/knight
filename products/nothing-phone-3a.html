Nothing Phone 3a In Kenya

Nothing Phone 3a Android Smartphone price in Kenya starts at Kshs 47,000. It was launched in March 2025. The phone runs on the Qualcomm SM7635 Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 (4 nm) processor with Adreno 710 (940 MHz) GPU. The phone has 12 GB RAM and 256 GB of internal storage which is not expandable. The Nothing Phone (3a) has a 6.77 inches, 1080 x 2392 pixels AMOLED display with a pixel density of 388 ppi. On the cameras, it has a 50 MP rear camera and a 32 MP front camera for selfies. It has a USB Type-C port for data syncing and charging. There is a fingerprint sensor present on the Nothing Phone (3a) as well, along with a magnetic field sensor (compass), gyroscope sensor, proximity sensor and accelerometer sensor. It is a single SIM smartphone with support for 3G, USB OTG, NFC and Wi-Fi too. The Nothing Phone (3a) runs on Android 15, up to 3 major Android upgrades and all this is powered by a 5000 mAh battery. It measures 163.5 x 77.5 x 8.4 mm (height x width x thickness) with a total weight of 201 grams including battery.

The Nothing Phone 3a is the latest addition to Nothing’s lineup, blending futuristic design, a unique LED Glyph Interface, and solid mid-range performance. Launched on March 4, 2025, the Nothing Phone 3a continues the brand’s minimalist aesthetic while packing a powerful Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 processor, a 120Hz AMOLED display, and a versatile triple-camera setup.

With an emphasis on software optimization, smooth performance, and an eye-catching design, this phone is aimed at users who want something different from the usual flagship clones. But does it deliver beyond its looks? Let’s find out.

Design and Build Quality

Dimensions and Weight

The Nothing Phone 3a measures 163.5 x 77.5 x 8.4 mm and weighs 201g. While slightly hefty, it offers a solid grip and a premium feel.

Materials and Build

The phone retains Nothing’s signature transparent aesthetic, with a semi-transparent rear panel that subtly reveals internal components. It is available in Black, White, and Milk, with a sleek and futuristic industrial design.

IP Rating

The device is IP64-rated, making it dust-tight and resistant to water splashes. While it can handle light exposure to moisture, it is not fully waterproof.

Display Quality

Display Type and Size

The Nothing Phone 3a features a 6.77-inch AMOLED display, offering vibrant colors and deep contrasts. The panel supports 1 billion colors, ensuring an immersive visual experience.

Color Reproduction

The AMOLED technology provides accurate color representation, making it ideal for media consumption, gaming, and content creation.

Refresh Rate

With a 120Hz refresh rate, the display ensures smooth scrolling, fluid animations, and a responsive user experience.

Brightness and Outdoor Visibility

With a peak brightness of 3000 nits, the screen remains clearly visible even in direct sunlight. The Always-On Display feature allows users to check notifications and time without unlocking the phone.

Performance and Software

Operating System

The Nothing Phone 3a runs on Android 15 with Nothing OS 3.1, providing a bloat-free, optimized user experience. The software is clean, highly customizable, and comes with a commitment to three major Android updates.

Chipset

The device is powered by the Qualcomm SM7635 Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 (4nm) chipset, designed for efficient performance and power management.

CPU and GPU Performance

The octa-core CPU configuration includes:

  • 1x 2.5 GHz Cortex-A720
  • 3x 2.4 GHz Cortex-A720
  • 4x 1.8 GHz Cortex-A520

The Adreno 710 GPU (940 MHz) ensures smooth gaming and multimedia performance. The combination allows for seamless multitasking and handles graphically demanding applications efficiently.

RAM and Storage

The Nothing Phone 3a comes in two configurations:

  • 8GB RAM with 128GB storage
  • 12GB RAM with 256GB storage

There is no microSD card slot, meaning users must rely on internal storage.

Camera Performance

Triple Rear Camera Setup

The phone features a versatile triple-camera system:

  • 50 MP (wide, f/1.9, OIS, PDAF)
  • 50 MP (telephoto, 2x optical zoom, PDAF)
  • 8 MP (ultrawide, f/2.2, 119° field of view)

Main Camera Performance

The 50 MP primary sensor delivers sharp, well-balanced images with optical image stabilization for reduced blur and improved low-light performance.

Secondary Cameras

The 50 MP telephoto lens provides 2x optical zoom, making it ideal for portrait shots and zoomed-in photography. The 8 MP ultrawide camera expands the field of view, capturing more content in a single frame.

Front Camera

The 32 MP front-facing camera, with an f/2.2 aperture, delivers high-quality selfies and smooth video calls.

Video Recording

The rear camera supports 4K recording at 30fps and 1080p at up to 120fps, with gyro-EIS ensuring stable video footage. The front camera records in 1080p at 30fps.

Battery and Charging

Battery Capacity

The Nothing Phone 3a is equipped with a 5000mAh non-removable Li-Po battery, offering all-day usage on a single charge.

Battery Life

The power-efficient 4nm chipset, combined with optimized software, ensures extended battery performance. The exact screen-on time may vary depending on usage.

Charging Capabilities

The device supports 50W wired charging, capable of reaching:

  • 50% in 19 minutes
  • 100% in 56 minutes

There is no wireless charging support.

Connectivity and Additional Features

Network Support

The Nothing Phone 3a supports:

  • 2G: GSM 850 / 900 / 1800 / 1900
  • 3G: HSDPA 800 / 850 / 900 / 1700(AWS) / 1900 / 2100
  • 4G: LTE
  • 5G: SA/NSA

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth

The device features Wi-Fi 6 for faster wireless connectivity and Bluetooth 5.4 for seamless pairing with accessories.

GPS and Navigation

The phone includes support for GPS, GALILEO, GLONASS, BDS, and QZSS, ensuring accurate location tracking.

USB Connectivity

The device uses USB Type-C 2.0 with OTG support for data transfer and charging.

Audio Features

The phone features stereo speakers but lacks a 3.5mm headphone jack, requiring users to rely on USB-C or wireless audio solutions.

Software Experience

UI Features

Nothing OS 3.1 offers a clean and optimized interface with unique design elements, including the Glyph Interface for notifications and alerts.

Software Updates

Nothing promises three major Android updates, ensuring long-term software support and security enhancements.

Pricing and Availability

Pricing

The Nothing Phone 3a is priced at Ksh 44,000 in Kenya.

Availability

The device is available for purchase at PhoneStore Kenya and other authorized retailers.

Final Thoughts

The Nothing Phone 3a stands out in the mid-range market with its unique design, efficient performance, and versatile camera system. It offers a fresh alternative to traditional smartphones while maintaining strong hardware and software support.

Pros and Cons

Pros:

  • Unique transparent design with LED Glyph Interface
  • Vibrant 6.77-inch AMOLED 120Hz display
  • Powerful Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chipset
  • Versatile triple-camera setup with 50 MP primary sensor
  • Fast 50W wired charging

Cons:

  • No expandable storage
  • No wireless charging
  • No 3.5mm headphone jack

Conclusion

The Nothing Phone 3a delivers an innovative design with strong mid-range specifications. It is well-suited for users looking for a stylish, capable smartphone with a clean software experience. While it lacks some flagship features, its performance, display, and unique LED Glyph Interface make it a compelling choice in its price range.
