Apple iPhone 15 Plus in Kenya

The Apple iPhone 15 Plus price in Kenya currently starts at Ksh 78,000. Apple iPhone 15 Plus was launched in September 2023 running on the Apple A16 Bionic (4 nm) processor with Apple GPU (5-core graphics) GPU. The phone has 6 GB RAM and 512 GB of internal storage which is not expandable. The iPhone 15 Plus has a 6.7-inch, 1290 x 2796 pixels display with a pixel density of 460 ppi.
 Coming to cameras, it has a 48 MP rear camera and a 12 MP front camera for selfies. It has a USB Type-C port for data syncing and charging. There is a magnetic field sensor (compass) present on the iPhone 15 Plus as well, along with a gyroscope sensor, proximity sensor, and accelerometer sensor. It is a dual SIM smartphone with support for 4G LTE, NFC, and Wi-Fi too. The iPhone 15 Plus runs on iOS 17. It measures 160.9 x 77.8 x 7.8 mm (height x width x thickness) with a total weight of 201 grams including battery.

iPhone 15 Plus Review

In September 2023, Apple held an event that launched the iPhone 15 series to the market. Tech enthusiasts in the market were eagerly waiting for the launch of the new phones by Apple. As they waited with excitement and anticipation we were all glad to see that Apple did not fail us a single bit. Because the joy and excitement that the iPhone 15 was absolutely worth the wait.

Join me today as I unveil the changes that Apple has introduced with the new iPhone 15 series. Today we will be particularly focusing on the Plus model. We will analyse any significate upgrades that the iPhone 15 Plus. Let us determine if this phone is worth the high price tag that it is assigned.

The iPhone 15 Plus’s main features include:

  • Powerful A16 Bionic chip: The A16 Bionic chip is the fastest smartphone chip in the world, delivering incredible performance for everything from gaming to photo editing.
  • Stunning new display: The new Super Retina XDR display on the iPhone 15 Plus is brighter, more colorful, and more energy-efficient than ever before. 
  • Advanced camera system: The iPhone 15 Plus features a new 48MP main camera sensor that captures incredible detail and clarity. It also has a new ultrawide camera with a wider field of view and a new telephoto camera with 2x optical zoom.
  • ProRes video recording: The iPhone 15 Plus is the first iPhone to support ProRes video recording, a professional-grade video format that’s perfect for filmmakers and content creators.
  • Long-lasting battery life: The iPhone 15 Plus has a larger battery than any previous iPhone, so you can stay powered up all day long

Design of the iPhone 15 Plus

  • Display: 6.7-inch Super Retina XDR display 
  • Material and Construction: Aluminum and glass, with a durable Ceramic Shield front cover
  • Dimensions and Weight: 160.84 mm x 78.1 mm x 7.81 mm, 203 grams
  • Color: Midnight, Starlight, Product(RED), Purple, Green

One thing that we noted is that last year, Apple did away with the mini iPhones. Instead, they replaced them with more expensive phone models. As a result, we saw the birth of the iPhone 14 Plus, this phone had a 6.7-inch display screen. The overall design material of the iPhone 14 Plus was a metallic and glass body. I was happy to see the iPhone 15 Plus following the same footsteps with even thinner bezels.

One thing that we were happy about was to see that Apple still maintained the design. One other notable difference that we see in the design is the pill-shaped aperture.  This was the transformation that Apple included coupled with the fact that the phone. iPhone 15 Plus comes with a dynamic Island that makes this model resemble last year’s Pro models. We were happy to see that the phone had been built with the same aluminum framework.

Apple also ensured that the Lightning port has been supplanted by a USB-C interface. When you look at the back of the phone you will see a rearglass that has a matte finish texture. Giving you a beautiful visual and enhancing your experience.

Display

Besides the functionality that you get with the Dynamic Island aperture display. It’s surprising how much the iPhone 15 Plus resembles the forerunner. Except for the fact that it comes with a ProMotion capability and an ever-present mode. Aside from that the iPhone 15 Plus comes with a 6.7-inch Super Retina XDR OLED screen with a 60Hz refresh rate.

The display also comes with a screen resolution of 2796×1290 pixels and a pixel density of 460 PPI. On the bright side, the iPhone 15 Plus boasts enhanced luminosity. The phone also flaunts a peak HDR brightness of 1600 nits and an outdoor brightness peak of up to 2000 nits.

Camera Performance of the iPhone 15 Plus

Main camera:

  • 48MP sensor
  • 12MP Ultrawide
  • New lens with a wider aperture
  • Optical image stabilization (OIS)
  • Cinematic mode for video recording

Selfies:

  • 12MP sensor
  • Wider field of view

Video:

  • 4K video recording at up to 60 frames per second
  • ProRes video recording support
  • Cinematic mode for video recording

We were happy to see that Apple included a dual camera on the back. This camera consists of a primary wide-angle lens and a secondary ultrawide lens. One main alternative is the inclusion of a 48MP primary camera, inherited from the Pro models. This upgraded primary camera captures 12MP images through pixel binning.

In low-light scenarios, the camera performance will make you happy. It’s important to note that the iPhone 15 Plus lacks the capability to capture photos in 48MP ProRaw mode. This is a feature reserved for the Pro models. Nevertheless, it offers a 2x sensor zoom on the primary camera. This feature has been denoted as “Optical-quality 2x telephoto,” delivering impressive portrait photographs. The ultrawide camera remains unaltered from the iPhone 14 Plus. This offers no major surprises.

Also, the selfie camera is the same as the one for last year. The phone comes with a 12-megapixel sensor that has features such as Portrait mode, Night mode, and 4K video recording at 60FPS.

Performance of the iPhone 15 Plus

  • Processor: A16 Bionic chip
  • GPU: Apple GPU (5-core graphics)
  • RAM: 6GB
  • Storage: 128GB, 256GB, 512GB, or 1TB
  • OS: iOS 17

You gotta love Apple for its continuous strategy. The way they differentiate regular and Plus models from the Pro versions is by using a slightly older chipset in the iPhone 15 Plus. It’s powered by the A16 Bionic chip, which was also in last year’s Pro models. This chip is still very capable and competitive with the latest ones from Qualcomm, Exynos, and MediaTek. (Although the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 performs better in certain gaming tests.) There don’t appear to be any noticeable changes in the RAM or storage capacity.

The iPhone 15 Plus still has 6GB of RAM and comes in three storage options: 128GB, 256GB, and 512 GB. Apple recently unveiled iOS 17 at its annual conference, which brings several exciting new features to the iPhone. The iPhone 15 Plus benefits from this fresh mobile OS, arriving pre-equipped with iOS 17.

Battery of the iPhone 15 Plus

  • Battery capacity: 4383 mAh
  • Charging speed: 30W fast charging
  • New A16 Bionic chip for improved energy efficiency

Renowned for its remarkable battery life, the iPhone 14 Plus set lofty standards. The iPhone 15 Plus continues the trend with a battery size of 4,325mAh, like the previous model. It also comes with a more efficient 4nm A16 Bionic chip. While Apple hasn’t given exact battery capacity details. One thing is certain, we can expect it to last as long as the previous version.

Charging

Even though the iPhone now uses USB-C, charging speed remains the same. Apple claims that you get a 50% charge in 30 minutes using a 20W fast charger. The iPhone 15 Plus adheres to USB 2.0 speeds, reserving the USB 3.0 standard for the Pro models. The phone still works with MagSafe and wireless charging, so you can use accessories that have been around since the iPhone 12 series came out.

Audio Quality

The iPhone 14 Plus received praise for its great audio quality and stereo setup. We are happy to inform you this trend continues with the iPhone 15 Plus. The larger size of the iPhone 15 Plus enhances the bass and sound quality, making the listening experience even better. The haptic feedback mechanism on this model continues to astound.

Apple iPhone 15 Plus Summary

The iPhone 15 Plus is a great phone with a lot to offer. Here is a summary of its key features, benefits, and why it’s a perfect fit for the target audience:

Key features:

  • Powerful A16 Bionic chip
  • Beautiful 6.7-inch Super Retina XDR display 
  • Advanced camera system with a 48MP main sensor, a 12MP ultrawide sensor, and cinematic mode
  • Large 4383 mAh battery with 15W fast charging support
  • Durable Ceramic Shield front cover
  • Water and dust resistance to an IP68 rating
  • iOS 17 with a new lock screen, a new Live Text feature, and a new Focus mode

Benefits:

  • The iPhone 15 Plus is one of the fastest and most powerful phones on the market.
  • The iPhone 15 Plus has a beautiful and immersive display.
  • The iPhone 15 Plus has a versatile camera system that can take stunning photos and videos in any situation.
  • The iPhone 15 Plus has a long-lasting battery that can easily get you through a full day of use.
  • The iPhone 15 Plus is durable and water-resistant, so you can use it with confidence in any environment.
  • The iPhone 15 Plus runs iOS 17, the latest version of Apple’s mobile operating system, which offers a number of new features and improvements.

Conclusion

The decision to buy the iPhone 15 Plus hinges on a multitude of considerations. If you own an iPhone 14 Plus, upgrading may not be a compelling proposition, but if you’re upgrading from an older iPhone, your decision will depend on whether you prefer a larger screen. If you love using Android, then consider switching to Apple. Because the iPhone 15 Plus is an enticing choice because it now uses USB-C, which means you might not need as many extra cables and chargers. The iPhone 15 Plus will be available in stores starting September 22, and it starts at a price of Ksh 78,000 the same as the previous model.
