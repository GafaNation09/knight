Design and Build Quality

The Infinix GT (2026) features a slim yet solid design, measuring 162.4 x 77.2 x 7.4 mm and weighing 201.7 grams. Despite packing a large battery and high-performance internals, the device maintains a relatively thin profile, giving it a premium and comfortable in-hand feel.

The build quality stands out with a combination of Gorilla Glass 7i on the front, an aluminum frame, and an aluminum back, making it more durable and premium compared to plastic-bodied competitors. The phone is also rated IP64, meaning it is dust-tight and resistant to water splashes, offering basic protection for everyday use.

A unique highlight is the RGB notification lighting system and active matrix display on the back. This gives the phone a distinctive gaming aesthetic while also serving functional purposes such as notifications, charging indicators, and system alerts.

The device supports dual Nano-SIM plus eSIM functionality, allowing flexible network usage while maintaining dual active lines.

Color options include Mist Titanium, Deep Ocean Blue, Solar Orange, Mocha Brown, Torino Black, and Frost Silver, giving users a wide range of bold and premium finishes.

Display Experience

The phone features a large 6.78-inch AMOLED display designed to deliver a highly immersive and smooth visual experience. AMOLED technology ensures deep blacks, vibrant colors, and improved power efficiency.

The 144Hz refresh rate provides ultra-smooth scrolling and fluid animations, making it ideal for gaming and fast-paced interactions. Combined with 2304Hz PWM dimming, the display significantly reduces eye strain during prolonged usage, especially in low-light environments.

With a resolution of 1208 x 2644 pixels and a pixel density of approximately 429 ppi, the screen delivers sharp text, detailed images, and high-quality video playback. Ultra HDR support further enhances color and brightness accuracy when viewing supported content.

Brightness levels are impressive, reaching 1600 nits in high brightness mode and peaking at 4500 nits, ensuring excellent visibility even under direct sunlight.

Performance and Software

At its core, the device is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset built on a 4nm process. This chipset is designed to deliver a balance of high performance and energy efficiency.

The octa-core CPU setup includes a Cortex-A720 prime core clocked at 2.7 GHz, supported by additional performance and efficiency cores that ensure smooth multitasking, gaming, and everyday operations.

The Adreno 810 GPU provides strong graphical performance, allowing the phone to handle modern mobile games, high frame rates, and demanding visual tasks with ease.

Running on Android 16 with XOS 16, the phone offers a refined user experience with enhanced customization, improved system stability, and gaming-focused optimizations.

Storage options include 256GB paired with either 8GB or 12GB RAM. While the device uses UFS 2.2 storage instead of newer standards, it still delivers reliable speeds for everyday use. There is no support for expandable storage.

Camera Capabilities

The Infinix GT (2026) comes with a dual rear camera setup designed for versatile photography. The 50MP primary sensor features a wide f/1.6 aperture along with PDAF and OIS, enabling sharp images, faster focusing, and improved low-light performance.

The 8MP ultrawide camera expands the field of view to 112 degrees, making it suitable for landscape shots and group photography, though it is more basic compared to flagship ultrawide sensors.

Camera features such as HDR and panorama enhance image quality by improving dynamic range and allowing wider scene capture.

For video recording, the device supports 4K at 30fps and up to 120fps at 1080p, making it suitable for both high-resolution recording and slow-motion capture.

On the front, the 13MP selfie camera delivers clear images and supports 4K video recording, which is a notable upgrade for users who create content or frequently engage in video calls.

Audio and Multimedia

The device is equipped with stereo speakers tuned by JBL, delivering richer sound quality with better bass and clarity. This enhances the experience when watching movies, gaming, or listening to music.

Although there is no 3.5mm headphone jack, the phone supports 24-bit/192kHz Hi-Res audio and Hi-Res wireless audio, ensuring excellent sound quality through compatible wired and wireless devices.

Connectivity and Network Support

The Infinix GT (2026) supports GSM, HSPA, LTE, and 5G networks, ensuring fast and reliable connectivity across different regions and network conditions.

It includes Wi-Fi 6 for faster and more stable wireless internet connections, along with Bluetooth 5.4 for improved efficiency and audio quality. NFC support allows for contactless payments and quick device pairing.

The device also includes an infrared port, enabling it to function as a remote control for compatible electronics, and even retains an FM radio feature, which is becoming rare in modern smartphones.

A USB Type-C 2.0 port with OTG support handles charging and data transfer.

Battery and Charging Technology

The phone offers either a 6000mAh or 6500mAh battery, both of which are designed to provide extended usage even under heavy workloads such as gaming or streaming.

Charging is one of its biggest highlights. With 90W wired fast charging, the device can reach 50% in as little as 14–16 minutes and a full charge in under 45 minutes depending on the battery variant.

It also supports 30W wireless charging, which adds convenience for users who prefer cable-free charging.

Additional features include reverse wired charging at 7.5W and reverse wireless charging at 5W, allowing the phone to power accessories like earbuds or other devices.

Bypass charging is another standout feature, especially for gamers, as it allows the phone to draw power directly from the charger without stressing the battery, reducing heat and prolonging battery lifespan.

Sensors and Extra Features

Security is handled by an under-display optical fingerprint sensor, ensuring quick and secure unlocking. The phone also includes a comprehensive set of sensors such as an accelerometer, gyroscope, compass, and proximity sensor.

Unique additions like a heart rate sensor and SpO2 monitoring provide extra health tracking capabilities, which are rarely found in smartphones within this segment.

Price in Kenya and Availability

The Infinix GT (2026) is expected to launch in Kenya soon after its global release in February 2026. Pricing is anticipated to fall within the upper mid-range category, depending on the RAM and battery configuration.

For Kenyan buyers, availability will be through Phones Store Kenya, where it is expected to offer strong value given its gaming features, premium build, and fast charging capabilities.

Final Verdict

The Infinix GT (2026) is a powerful and feature-rich smartphone built for performance enthusiasts. Its combination of a 144Hz AMOLED display, Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset, massive battery options, and ultra-fast charging makes it an excellent choice for gamers and heavy users.

The addition of RGB lighting, bypass charging, and JBL-tuned audio further reinforces its identity as a gaming-focused device. For users in Kenya looking for a stylish, high-performance smartphone with standout features, this model is set to be a very compelling option once it becomes available.
