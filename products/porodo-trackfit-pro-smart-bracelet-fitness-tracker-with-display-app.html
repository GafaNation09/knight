Design and Comfort

The Porodo TrackFit Pro’s zinc alloy and ABS plastic construction provides a premium metal-accent look with the lightweight practicality of plastic, combining the visual quality of a metallic finish with the comfort advantage of a low total weight. At 27.2 grams, the TrackFit Pro is one of the lightest wearables reviewed in Phonesstore Kenya’s product range, sitting significantly lighter than the Samsung Galaxy Watch9 at 34 grams and far lighter than the Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra2 at 61.5 grams.

The 9.5mm case thickness is among the slimmest wearable profiles in Phonesstore Kenya’s range, sitting comfortably under shirt cuffs, long sleeves, and formal attire without creating a visible bump or snagging on fabric during everyday movement. The 24.5mm case width is the narrow bracelet profile that distinguishes fitness bands from the larger watch-style smartwatches, sitting closer to a traditional bracelet aesthetic on the wrist for buyers who find larger smartwatch case diameters uncomfortable or visually disproportionate.

The Silver colour provides a clean, professional metallic finish that complements both casual and formal dress codes, making the TrackFit Pro suitable for office, gym, and everyday outdoor wear without drawing attention as a fitness device.

Health Monitoring

The heart rate monitoring provides continuous heart rate data throughout the day and during exercise sessions, enabling training zone awareness, resting heart rate trend monitoring, and post-workout recovery tracking through the BR Fit companion app. The blood oxygen SpO2 monitoring measures the percentage of oxygen in the blood, providing a health metric relevant for altitude awareness, sleep quality assessment, and general respiratory health monitoring.

Sleep tracking monitors the duration and basic patterns of nightly sleep, providing the data that most buyers use the BR Fit app to review as a morning health check on sleep quality and duration. Multiple exercise modes cover the common workout types that everyday fitness users engage in including running, cycling, walking, and other activities, with each mode providing the most relevant metrics for that specific activity type.

Step counting, distance tracking, and calorie monitoring provide the daily activity baseline that most fitness tracker buyers want as an accountability tool for daily movement goals, encouraging active behaviour and enabling progress tracking over weeks and months of daily wear.

Connectivity and App

Bluetooth 5.2 is the latest Bluetooth standard for reliable, low-power pairing with compatible Android 8.0 and above and iOS 12.0 and above smartphones, covering all current Android and iPhone models available in Kenya. The BR Fit companion app receives health data from the TrackFit Pro for review, trend analysis, and historical comparison, presenting the heart rate, SpO2, sleep, and activity data in a browsable format on the paired smartphone.

Smartphone notification mirroring displays incoming call alerts, messages, and app notifications on the TrackFit Pro’s display, allowing the wearer to see notification previews without reaching for their phone during exercise sessions, meetings, and outdoor activities.

Battery Life and Charging

The 145mAh battery delivering up to 10 days of working time reduces the charging frequency that smartwatch alternatives require from every one to two days to approximately weekly, removing battery anxiety as a daily concern. The 15 to 20-day standby time provides extended reserve for periods of low activity monitoring usage.

The magnetic charging system attaches the charging connector to the back of the band without a physical port, providing convenient, tool-free charging that requires only placing the magnetic charging contact against the designated point on the back of the tracker. The approximately 2-hour full charge time covers overnight charging and provides a full 10-day battery from a single evening charge.

Pros and Cons

The Porodo TrackFit Pro has clear strengths at KSh 8,000. It delivers heart rate monitoring, blood oxygen SpO2, sleep tracking, multiple exercise modes, step counting, distance tracking, calorie monitoring, smartphone notifications, Bluetooth 5.2, IP68 water resistance, a 145mAh battery at 10-day working time and 20-day standby, magnetic charging, a 2-hour full charge, zinc alloy and ABS construction, 9.5mm thin profile, approximately 27.2-gram weight, and Android and iOS compatibility at KSh 8,000.

Its main limitations are the basic digital display that is less visually rich than the AMOLED displays on the Amazfit GTS 4 Mini at KSh 15,000 and the Infinix XWatch N5 Pro at KSh 6,000, the absence of built-in GPS requiring a connected smartphone for outdoor route tracking, the limited smartwatch functionality without apps or contactless payments, the single Silver colour option without colour variety, and the smaller 24.5mm case width that displays less information simultaneously than wider smartwatch alternatives.
