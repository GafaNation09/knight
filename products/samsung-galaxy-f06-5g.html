Samsung Galaxy F06 5G Price in Kenya

The Samsung Galaxy F06 5G price in Kenya is KES 20,000. It is a budget-friendly 5G smartphone designed to bring next-generation connectivity, long software support, and reliable everyday performance to entry-level users. Despite its affordable pricing, it offers a large display, a capable 5G chipset, and Samsung’s signature software ecosystem, making it a strong value option in the budget segment.

The Galaxy F06 5G features a 6.7-inch HD+ display with a 90Hz refresh rate, delivering smooth scrolling and a wide viewing experience for streaming, browsing, and social media use. In addition, the display reaches up to 800 nits (HBM) brightness, ensuring decent outdoor visibility for a phone in this category.

Under the hood, the device is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 6300 5G chipset built on a 6nm process, paired with up to 6GB RAM and 128GB internal storage. As a result, it delivers stable performance for everyday tasks such as messaging, video streaming, light gaming, and multitasking.

The camera system consists of a 50MP main sensor supported by a 2MP depth sensor, enabling basic photography with portrait capabilities and decent daylight shots. On the front, an 8MP selfie camera handles video calls and social media content.

Battery life is one of the strongest aspects of the Galaxy F06 5G, featuring a 5000mAh battery supported by 25W fast charging. This ensures all-day usage with relatively fast top-ups when needed.

The phone runs on Android 15 with Samsung’s One UI Core 7.0 and is backed by up to 4 major Android upgrades, which is a rare advantage in this price segment. Additional features such as Samsung Knox Vault, a side-mounted fingerprint sensor, and expandable storage up to 1TB further enhance its value proposition.

The Samsung Galaxy F06 5G is available in Bahama Blue and Lit Violet, and is now in stock at Phones Store Kenya.
