Beats Studio Pro Headphones Price in Kenya

The Beats Studio Pro Headphones price in Kenya is KSh 26,000, bringing Beats’ flagship over-ear headphones with active noise cancelling, Personalized Spatial Audio, and up to 40 hours of battery life. They work well with both Apple and Android devices, and they add something many wireless headphones lack: lossless audio through a USB-C cable.

Sound and Noise Cancelling

Beats’ redesigned drivers and adaptive ANC make everyday listening more immersive.

40mm Drivers with Active Noise Cancelling

Custom 40mm dynamic drivers deliver balanced, detailed sound, while fully adaptive Active Noise Cancelling reduces background noise and a Transparency mode lets you hear your surroundings when you need to.

Personalized Spatial Audio

Personalized Spatial Audio with dynamic head tracking creates a more immersive sound for music, movies, and games on compatible Apple devices.

Lossless Audio via USB-C

A built-in DAC delivers lossless audio (up to 24-bit/48kHz) when you connect with the included USB-C cable, with three sound profiles to choose from: Beats Signature, Entertainment, and Conversation. You can also plug in with the 3.5mm analog cable for in-flight entertainment and other wired sources.

Connectivity and Calls

Class 1 Bluetooth 5.3

Class 1 Bluetooth 5.3 gives strong wireless range and a stable connection. The headphones support SBC and AAC codecs and can connect to two devices at once, such as a phone and a laptop.

One-Touch Pairing for Apple and Android

One-touch pairing and Find My work with Apple devices, and Google Fast Pair and the Beats app give Android users a smooth setup and full control.

Clearer Calls

Improved microphones with beamforming help your voice stay clear on calls, even in busy places.

Battery Life

Up to 40 Hours

The headphones deliver up to 40 hours of battery life with ANC off and around 24 hours with ANC on. Fast Fuel gives about 4 hours of playback from a 10-minute charge, and they charge through USB-C.

Design and Comfort

UltraPlush Leather Cushions

Soft UltraPlush leather cushions keep the ear cups comfortable, and the lightweight, foldable design (about 260g) packs easily for travel. The headphones come in Black, Deep Brown, Navy, and Sandstone.

What’s in the Box

The package includes the Beats Studio Pro headphones, a woven carrying case, a USB-C to USB-C cable, a 3.5mm audio cable, and a quick start guide with a warranty card.
