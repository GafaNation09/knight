Design and Build Quality

The Samsung Galaxy A27 makes a strong impression for a phone at KES 34,500. The combination of Gorilla Glass Victus+ on both the front and back is an unusual premium touch for a mid-range device — most phones at this price use glass only on the front and plastic on the back. Gorilla Glass Victus+ offers improved scratch and drop resistance over standard glass, giving the A27 a more durable and premium feel in everyday use.

At 7.8mm thin and 200 grams, it sits comfortably in hand and pocket without feeling heavy despite the large display and battery inside. The four colour options — Black, Blue, Light Green, and Light Pink — cover a range of personalities from understated professional to fresh and expressive. The plastic frame keeps the weight manageable while the glass sandwich construction gives the phone its premium look and feel.

The side-mounted fingerprint sensor is well-positioned for natural thumb or index finger placement depending on how you hold the phone, and virtual proximity sensing replaces the traditional proximity sensor for a cleaner internal design. IP64 dust and splash resistance adds practical protection against the dust and occasional rain that are part of everyday life in Kenya.

The EU Energy Label data reveals some impressive durability credentials: the battery is rated for 1200 charge cycles — significantly more than the 500-cycle industry standard — and the phone carries a Class B free-fall drop rating covering 180 drops. This speaks to Samsung’s commitment to the Galaxy A27 lasting well beyond the first year or two of use.

Display Quality

The 6.7-inch Super AMOLED display is the most immediately impressive aspect of the Samsung Galaxy A27. Super AMOLED panels produce deep blacks, vivid colours, and excellent contrast by lighting each pixel individually rather than using a backlight, making them fundamentally better than the IPS LCD panels found on competing phones at this price in Kenya.

The 120Hz refresh rate ensures smooth scrolling, fluid animations, and responsive touch input across everything from social media to gaming. At 385 ppi, the Full HD+ resolution is sharp enough for comfortable reading, video streaming, and detailed photo review. Corning Gorilla Glass Victus+ provides meaningful scratch resistance rated to Mohs level 5, protecting the display from keys, coins, and everyday pocket debris.

For everyday use — streaming YouTube, scrolling Instagram, reading documents, or watching Netflix — this is a premium-feeling display that punches well above the phone’s price point.

Camera Performance

Rear Camera System

The Samsung Galaxy A27 features a 50MP primary camera with f/1.8 aperture, OIS, and PDAF. At this price point, the inclusion of Optical Image Stabilisation is notable — it reduces blur caused by hand movement during stills and smooths out handheld video in a way that software stabilisation alone cannot replicate. PDAF ensures fast, accurate focus lock on subjects, including moving people and objects.

The 1/2.76-inch sensor is a respectable size for a mid-range camera, capturing more light than the smaller sensors found in many competing phones. This pays dividends in lower light conditions, where the wider light-gathering capability and f/1.8 aperture work together to produce cleaner, more detailed results.

The 5MP ultrawide lens adds creative flexibility for group shots, landscapes, and tight indoor spaces where a standard wide lens cannot fit the full scene. The 2MP macro lens allows close-up photography of small objects, textures, and detail shots. Video capability reaches 4K at 30fps for main recording and 720p at 480fps for slow-motion footage, with gyro-EIS providing additional stabilisation.

Selfie Camera

The 12MP front camera with autofocus is a significant upgrade over the fixed-focus selfie cameras common at this price range. Autofocus ensures sharp selfies even when the subject is not at the ideal distance from the lens, and 12MP resolution provides enough detail for social media, profile photos, and video calls. It records video at 1080p at 30fps.

Performance and Software

The Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 built on a 4nm process is a capable and efficient mid-range chipset that handles everyday tasks including social media, streaming, navigation, WhatsApp, and multitasking smoothly. The Adreno 710 GPU supports moderate gaming comfortably, covering popular titles at good settings without significant frame drops.

With 6GB or 8GB RAM depending on the configuration and UFS storage, the Galaxy A27 manages app switching and background processes without the slowdowns that affect entry-level phones. The 256GB storage option gives users generous room for photos, videos, apps, and files, with microSDXC expansion via the shared SIM slot for additional space as needed.

The software story is where the Galaxy A27 truly stands out at its price. It ships with Android 16 and Samsung’s One UI 8.5 — already the latest versions available — and Samsung has committed to six major Android OS upgrades. This means the A27 will receive updates until approximately 2032, which is an extraordinary support commitment for a phone at KES 34,500 and places it ahead of most competitors in its price range on longevity alone.

One UI 8.5 brings Samsung’s full suite of features including Samsung DeX, which allows the Galaxy A27 to function as a desktop computer when connected to a monitor via USB-C, offering a full desktop interface with app windows and keyboard and mouse support. This is a feature normally associated with Samsung’s premium Galaxy S and Z series, making its presence on the A27 a meaningful productivity bonus.

Battery Life and Charging

The 5000mAh battery is well-matched to the Galaxy A27’s display and chipset, delivering a comfortable full day of heavy use and often extending into a second day for moderate users. Samsung’s EU Energy Label data rates the battery endurance at 51 hours and 10 minutes under its standardised testing methodology, and the battery is rated for 1200 charge cycles — more than double the 500-cycle standard common in the industry — which means the battery should retain strong capacity well into the phone’s multi-year lifespan.

The 25W wired fast charging brings the battery to 45 percent in 30 minutes, which is practical for a quick top-up before leaving home or the office. It is not the fastest charging available in this price range, but it is adequate for a daily routine and significantly faster than the 15W and 18W chargers that come with many competing phones.

Pros and Cons

The Samsung Galaxy A27 has clear and meaningful strengths. It offers a 6.7-inch 120Hz Super AMOLED display with Gorilla Glass Victus+ on both front and back, six major Android OS upgrade commitments, a 50MP OIS triple camera system with 4K video, a 12MP autofocus selfie camera, a 5000mAh battery rated for 1200 charge cycles, Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 on 4nm, Samsung DeX desktop support, eSIM compatibility, IP64 protection, a side-mounted fingerprint sensor, and microSDXC storage expansion — all at KES 34,500.

Its main limitations are the absence of a 3.5mm headphone jack, 25W charging that is slower than some competitors in this segment, a USB Type-C 2.0 port rather than the faster 3.1 or 3.2 standard, NFC and FM Radio status that is currently unspecified, the shared microSDXC and SIM slot which requires choosing between dual physical SIM and expanded storage, and a 5MP ultrawide that is modest by mid-range standards.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Samsung Galaxy A27 price in Kenya?

The Samsung Galaxy A27 price in Kenya is KES 34,500. It is available in two configurations: 128GB with 6GB RAM and 256GB with 8GB RAM. You can buy it from Phonesstore Kenya with nationwide delivery.

Does the Samsung Galaxy A27 support 5G?

Yes, the Samsung Galaxy A27 supports 5G alongside 4G LTE, 3G, and 2G. It is also known as the Samsung Galaxy A27 5G. 5G availability depends on network rollout in Kenya, but the phone is fully ready when coverage expands.

How many years of software updates does the Samsung Galaxy A27 receive?

Samsung has committed to six major Android OS upgrades for the Galaxy A27, starting from Android 16 at launch. This means the phone will receive major Android updates until approximately 2032, making it one of the most future-proof mid-range smartphones available in Kenya at this price.

Does the Samsung Galaxy A27 support eSIM?

Yes. The Galaxy A27 supports both Nano-SIM + eSIM and Nano-SIM + Nano-SIM configurations, giving users flexibility to manage two numbers with or without a physical second SIM card.

Does the Samsung Galaxy A27 support Samsung DeX?

Yes. The Galaxy A27 supports Samsung DeX, which allows you to connect the phone to an external monitor via USB-C and use it as a desktop computer with a full windowed interface. A keyboard and mouse can be connected for a complete desktop experience.

Is the Samsung Galaxy A27 waterproof?

The Galaxy A27 carries an IP64 rating, which means it is fully dust tight and protected against water splashes from any direction. It is not rated for submersion and should not be submerged in water, but it handles everyday splashes, light rain, and accidental spills comfortably.

Does the Samsung Galaxy A27 have a headphone jack?

No. The Galaxy A27 does not include a 3.5mm headphone jack. You can use USB-C headphones, a USB-C to 3.5mm adapter, or Bluetooth audio with Bluetooth 5.1 support.

Is the Samsung Galaxy A27 good for gaming?

Yes, for mid-range gaming. The Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 with Adreno 710 GPU handles popular titles including PUBG Mobile, Call of Duty Mobile, and FIFA Mobile comfortably. The 120Hz Super AMOLED display adds visual smoothness during gameplay, and the 5000mAh battery ensures long gaming sessions without needing a charge.
