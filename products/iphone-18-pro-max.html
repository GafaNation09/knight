Design and Build Quality

The most immediately noticeable physical change isn’t visual, it’s tactile: the iPhone 18 Pro Max is 16 grams heavier than its predecessor, a real, felt difference in hand that comes directly from the redesigned, larger battery cell required to hit the 45-hour rating.

The smaller Dynamic Island is the confirmed visual update on the front, now capable of displaying up to three Live Activities simultaneously, a meaningful functional upgrade for anyone tracking multiple things at once, a food delivery and a sports score, for instance, rather than just a cosmetic shrink. Black returns to the colour lineup after the iPhone 17 Pro skipped it, while Cosmic Orange has been discontinued in favor of Burgundy, a deep red-toned finish Apple settled on for its actual branding.

Display Quality

The display specifications carry forward largely unchanged in raw numbers from the iPhone 17 Pro Max: 6.9 inches, 1320 x 2868 pixels, roughly 460 ppi, and a 3000-nit peak brightness ceiling. What’s confirmed as new is the smaller Dynamic Island’s expanded multi-activity display capability, a software and hardware pairing that makes the reclaimed screen space genuinely functional rather than purely cosmetic. Both HDR10 and Dolby Vision support continue unchanged from the prior generation.

Performance and the A20 Pro

The 7-core GPU is rated up to 40 percent faster than the A19 Pro’s, and a dual 16-core Neural Engine doubles the AI processing power of last year’s chip, both genuinely large single-generation claims. The C2 modem, meanwhile, uses roughly 15 percent less energy than its C1X predecessor while adding mmWave 5G support in the US, a combination that feeds directly into the battery story: a modem that draws less power for the same connectivity task is one of the three confirmed contributors to this year’s battery gain, alongside the larger cell and the more efficient 2nm chip itself.

Camera Performance

The 48MP Fusion Main camera’s variable aperture remains the standout hardware change, a genuinely mechanical upgrade rather than a computational one, letting the lens physically adjust between f/1.5 and f/4.0 depending on the shot rather than relying on a single fixed opening and software processing to compensate. Alongside it, Apple introduced several camera software features specific to this generation: Smart Focus subject tracking, a faster Night mode, Cinematic effects now running at up to 60fps rather than the 30fps ceiling of prior models, and 4K Dolby Vision HDR time-lapse, a genuinely new capture mode.

The addition of manual shutter speed, aperture, white balance, and exposure controls directly inside Apple’s own Camera app continues to be one of the more significant software-side changes this cycle, moving functionality that historically required a third-party app like Halide into Apple’s native experience.

Battery Life and Charging

This is unambiguously the headline of the entire release, and it’s worth walking through Apple’s three published ratings individually rather than treating “45 hours” as the whole story. Video playback, the traditional and most favorable metric Apple publishes, sits at up to 45 hours for the Pro Max. Streamed video, which factors in the additional power cost of an active network connection, drops to up to 40 hours. Typical use, a newer metric Apple introduced this year based on median usage across messaging, photos, music, social apps, streaming, games, browsing, and navigation, is rated at up to 30 hours on a full charge, arguably the most realistic figure for how the phone will actually perform across a genuine day of mixed use.

Pros and Cons

The Apple iPhone 18 Pro Max has genuinely confirmed, announcement-day strengths at KSh 202,000. It delivers the longest battery life ever in an iPhone at up to 45 hours of video playback, a six-hour generational gain over the 17 Pro Max, the new A20 Pro on a 2nm process with a 40% faster GPU and doubled Neural Engine AI processing, a 48MP Fusion Main camera with genuine variable aperture plus new Smart Focus tracking and 4K Dolby Vision time-lapse, faster charging with a 15-minute 50% top-up, IP68 water resistance to 6 metres, 2TB of storage for the first time on an iPhone, and the new Apple C2 modem with mmWave 5G support in the US, at KSh 202,000.

Its main limitations are a confirmed 16-gram weight increase over its predecessor, Apple’s battery ratings applying only to eSIM-only models with no published figures for physical-SIM variants, still-unpublished exact CPU clock speeds, a $100 price increase over the iPhone 17 Pro Max reflected globally, and the fact that no independent, hands-on battery or performance testing yet exists to verify Apple’s announcement-day figures.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the iPhone 18 Pro Max battery actually last?

Apple rates it at up to 45 hours of video playback, up to 40 hours of streamed video, and up to 30 hours of typical mixed use on a full charge, all figures applying to eSIM-only models specifically.

Does Apple confirm the exact battery capacity in mAh?

No. Apple has never published battery capacity in milliampere-hours for its Pro-tier iPhones. The commonly cited 5,567mAh figure for the eSIM-only Pro Max is an industry estimate, not an Apple-confirmed number.

What is the Apple iPhone 18 Pro Max price in Kenya?

The Apple iPhone 18 Pro Max is priced at KSh 202,000 through Phonesstore Kenya, ahead of the global September 18 release date.

What makes the A20 Pro chip different?

It’s Apple’s first iPhone chip built on a 2-nanometer process, pairing a 6-core CPU with a 7-core GPU Apple says is up to 40% faster than the A19 Pro, plus a dual 16-core Neural Engine that doubles AI processing power.

What is variable aperture, and why does it matter?

It’s a physically adjustable camera aperture ranging from f/1.5 to f/4.0, a mechanical feature rather than a software simulation, letting the 48MP Fusion Main camera widen for low light or narrow for deeper focus, a creative control not previously possible on an iPhone.

How much heavier is the iPhone 18 Pro Max than last year’s model?

16 grams heavier, a direct trade-off for the larger battery cell that enables the 45-hour video playback rating.
