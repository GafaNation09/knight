Design and Build Quality

The Tecno Pova Curve 2 achieves a genuinely impressive engineering accomplishment for a phone with an 8000mAh battery: at 7.4mm thin and 195 grams, it is slimmer and lighter than many competing phones with 5000mAh batteries. The slim 7.4mm profile challenges the assumption that a large-battery phone must be thick and heavy, making the Pova Curve 2 as comfortable in everyday carry as a conventional mid-range phone.

Three colour options, Mystic Purple, Melting Silver, and Storm Titanium, are premium-sounding finishes that reflect Tecno’s design ambition for the Pova Curve 2. Mystic Purple is the most expressive and fashion-forward choice. Melting Silver provides a sophisticated metallic warmth. Storm Titanium brings the premium material connotation of titanium to an accessible price point.

Gorilla Glass 7i on the display provides the strongest glass protection Tecno has applied to a Pova device, with Mohs level 4 scratch resistance against everyday abrasion. IP64 provides dust protection and water splash resistance, and the 1.5-metre drop resistance confirms structural confidence beyond the glass protection alone.

Bypass charging is a gaming-focused battery management feature that routes power from the charger directly to the phone’s processor during gaming sessions, bypassing the battery entirely. This prevents the heat accumulation that occurs when a battery is simultaneously charging and discharging under gaming load, protecting long-term battery health and preventing thermal throttling during extended gaming sessions.

Display Quality

The 6.78-inch AMOLED at 4500 nits peak brightness and 144Hz refresh rate is one of the most impressive display specifications at KES 55,500 in Kenya. The 4500 nit peak is the same brightness as the Motorola Edge 60 and Edge 70, and the 144Hz refresh rate goes beyond the 120Hz ceiling of most competing phones at this price, providing smoother gaming, scrolling, and animation at the highest frame rate available in this price tier in Kenya.

The 2304Hz PWM dimming is the highest PWM frequency of any phone reviewed in Phonesstore Kenya’s entire product range, exceeding the Nothing Phone (3a) Lite’s 2160Hz and the Realme 16 Pro’s 3840Hz. At 2304Hz, the display backlight flickering is completely imperceptible to all users, making the Pova Curve 2 the most eye-comfortable AMOLED in its price class for low-brightness use, particularly relevant for gaming and media consumption during evening hours.

At 429 ppi across 1080 x 2364 pixels, the display renders content with sharp, clear detail suitable for all everyday and entertainment use cases. The Gorilla Glass 7i provides premium display protection that is typically reserved for more expensive competing phones at this price point.

Camera Performance

The 50MP main camera with f/1.8 aperture and PDAF delivers detailed, focused everyday photography for social media, WhatsApp, and documentation use. PDAF provides fast, accurate focus, and 1440p video at 30fps provides higher-resolution video output than the 1080p ceiling on many competing phones at this price.

The 2MP macro lens adds close-up detail photography capability for product shots, nature photography, and creative close-up content. The 13MP selfie camera handles everyday selfies and video calls at a functional level for the target buyer.

Performance and Software

The MediaTek Dimensity 7100 on a 6nm process with integrated 5G handles everyday tasks, social media, streaming, web browsing, and mid-tier gaming smoothly. The four Cortex-A78 performance cores at 2.4GHz provide strong everyday responsiveness, and the Mali-G610 MC2 GPU handles popular gaming titles at good settings.

With up to 12GB RAM and up to 256GB UFS 2.2 storage, the Pova Curve 2 manages everyday multitasking and media libraries without storage pressure. Android 16 with HiOS 16 provides Tecno’s feature-rich interface. NFC enables contactless payments. The infrared port enables home appliance remote control, replacing TV, air conditioner, and fan remotes from the phone. FM radio provides local radio without data consumption. Bluetooth 5.4 covers current-generation wireless audio. OTG enables direct USB peripheral connection.

The 24-bit/192kHz Hi-Res Audio and Hi-Res Wireless specifications with Dolby Atmos stereo speakers provide a complete premium audio specification at this price, with wired Hi-Res output for compatible headphones and wireless Hi-Res for compatible Bluetooth audio devices.

Battery Life and Charging

The 8000mAh battery is the largest battery capacity of any smartphone reviewed in Phonesstore Kenya’s entire product range. For everyday users in Kenya, three to four days between charges is a realistic target under moderate use, and even heavy users who stream extensively, game, and maintain Bluetooth connectivity throughout the day can comfortably achieve two full days between charges.

The 45W wired charging reaches 50 percent in 36 minutes, providing practical fast top-up capability from the large battery cell. For a phone rated at 8000mAh, the 45W charging speed is adequate for the battery size, recovering meaningful capacity during lunch breaks, short rest periods, and desk charging sessions.

Bypass charging protects the battery during intensive gaming by routing charger power directly to the system, preventing the thermal stress and cycle count accumulation that simultaneous charge and discharge under gaming load creates. For heavy gamers who play for extended sessions while plugged in, bypass charging meaningfully extends the battery’s long-term health and capacity retention.

Pros and Cons

The Tecno Pova Curve 2 has exceptional strengths at KES 55,500. It delivers an 8000mAh battery, the largest in Phonesstore Kenya’s product range, a 6.78-inch AMOLED at 4500 nits and 144Hz with 2304Hz PWM dimming and Gorilla Glass 7i, 5G via Dimensity 7100, a 50MP PDAF camera with 1440p video, Dolby Atmos stereo speakers with 24-bit Hi-Res and Hi-Res Wireless audio, NFC, an infrared port, FM radio, bypass charging, IP64, 1.5-metre drop resistance, up to 12GB RAM with up to 256GB storage, Bluetooth 5.4, and Android 16 with HiOS 16 in a 7.4mm body at KES 55,500.

Its main limitations are the absence of OIS on the main camera, the 13MP selfie camera that is modest compared to the 32MP and 50MP selfie cameras on competing phones at KES 38,000 to KES 69,000 in Kenya, the lack of a microSD card slot for storage expansion, Wi-Fi 5 rather than Wi-Fi 6 or 6E, USB Type-C 2.0 without faster data transfer, the GPS without GALILEO, GLONASS, BDS, and QZSS multi-system positioning confirmation, the absence of a confirmed EU Energy Label battery endurance rating, and the HiOS 16 interface that is heavier than the near-stock interfaces of Motorola and Nothing OS alternatives.
