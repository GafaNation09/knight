OnePlus 16 Price in Kenya

The OnePlus 16 price in Kenya starts from KSh 168,000, bringing the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 chipset, a 200MP main camera, and a huge 9,000mAh battery to OnePlus’s latest flagship. With a 185Hz LTPO AMOLED display and 100W wired charging, it is built for power users, mobile gamers, and photographers who want top performance with battery life to match.

OnePlus 16 Display

A big, fast, and bright screen sets the tone for everything on this phone.

6.78-Inch 185Hz LTPO AMOLED

The OnePlus 16 has a 6.78-inch LTPO AMOLED display with a sharp 1272 x 2772 resolution, 1 billion colours, and an ultra-smooth 185Hz refresh rate. It supports Dolby Vision, HDR10+, and HDR Vivid, with 3840Hz PWM dimming to ease eye strain.

2,000 Nits Brightness and Slim Bezels

Brightness reaches 2,000 nits in high brightness mode for clear outdoor viewing. The screen has 1.15mm bezels, an ultrasonic fingerprint scanner, and is protected by Crystal Shield Glass.

Performance and Storage

Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6

The phone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 (2nm), with an octa-core CPU that peaks at 5.01GHz and an Adreno 850 GPU, delivering top-tier speed for gaming, multitasking, and demanding apps.

RAM and Storage Options

Choose from 12GB or 16GB of LPDDR5X Ultra RAM with 256GB, 512GB, or 1TB of UFS 4.1 storage. There is no microSD slot. It runs Android 17 with OxygenOS 17.

Camera System

200MP Main Camera

The triple rear camera has a 200MP main sensor (1/1.4-inch, f/1.6), a 50MP ultrawide (f/2.0), and a 50MP 3x telephoto (f/2.6). It records video at up to 8K.

50MP Selfie Camera

A 50MP front camera (f/2.0) captures detailed selfies and sharp video calls.

Battery and Charging

9,000mAh Silicon-Carbon Battery

The 9,000mAh silicon-carbon battery is a 23% jump over the OnePlus 15’s 7,300mAh cell, giving serious all-day and multi-day endurance.

100W Wired and 50W Wireless Charging

It supports 100W wired charging and 50W wireless charging, plus reverse wireless and reverse wired charging for topping up earbuds, watches, and other phones.

Design and Connectivity

Slim Build and Colours

It measures 8.5mm thick and weighs about 217g to 220g depending on the finish. It comes in Starlight, Dark Forest, and Martian Masterpiece.

5G and Dual SIM

The OnePlus 16 supports 5G and dual Nano SIM for fast, flexible connectivity.
