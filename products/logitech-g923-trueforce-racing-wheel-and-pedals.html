Logitech G923 TRUEFORCE Racing Wheel and Pedals Price in Kenya, Full Specifications and Review (2026)

The Logitech G923 TRUEFORCE Racing Wheel and Pedals price in Kenya is KSh 45,000. It is a complete racing setup for home sim racing, with a leather-wrapped steel wheel and a three-pedal set. Its headline feature is TRUEFORCE, Logitech’s high-definition force feedback, which connects to the game engine and updates up to 4,000 times a second. The wheel turns 900 degrees lock-to-lock with a Hall-effect steering sensor and dual-motor force feedback. The pedals include a nonlinear brake that mimics a real brake’s pressure feel. It comes in versions for PlayStation 5, PS4 and PC, or for Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One and PC.

A racing wheel changes how driving games feel. With a gamepad, steering is a small thumb movement and cornering is guesswork. With a wheel, players feel the car through their hands: tyres losing grip, the weight shifting through a corner, kerbs and bumps on the track. Force feedback pushes back as it would in a real car, which makes it easier to catch a slide and hit braking points consistently. That’s why the G923 suits fans of Gran Turismo, F1, Forza and other racing titles who want to go beyond a controller.

TRUEFORCE Force Feedback

Standard force feedback wheels push back on the steering based on what the game tells them about the car. TRUEFORCE goes further by connecting directly to the game engine’s physics and audio, processing data up to 4,000 times a second. In supported games, the wheel can convey finer detail: engine vibration, tyre texture and the difference between tarmac, gravel and kerbs. The dual-motor system with closed-loop control keeps the feedback smooth and precise.

TRUEFORCE needs game integration. In games without it, the G923 still works as a standard force feedback wheel, which most racing games that support Logitech wheels provide. An overheat safeguard protects the motors during long sessions.

Steering and Wheel Design

The wheel turns 900 degrees lock-to-lock, two and a half turns, close to many road cars. That lets players make fine steering adjustments rather than the twitchy inputs of a thumbstick. A Hall-effect sensor measures the wheel’s position with magnets rather than physical contact, which helps it stay accurate over time without the wear of contact-based sensors. The brushed metal construction and hand-stitched leather cover give a premium feel and a comfortable grip.

On-wheel buttons and a 24-point selector let players adjust settings such as traction control or brake bias without leaving the track. Colour RPM LEDs sit in the driver’s line of sight to show engine speed and signal shift points in supported games.

Pedals: Nonlinear Brake and Dual Clutch

The three polished metal pedals cover throttle, brake and clutch. The nonlinear brake pedal is the standout: instead of moving evenly, it gets firmer as it’s pressed, imitating the pressure-based feel of a real brake. Braking by pressure rather than pedal travel helps players brake more consistently, a key skill in sim racing. The pedals are self-calibrating, use a patented carpet grip and textured heel rest to stay in place, and have six M6 mount points for bolting to a racing rig.

The programmable dual clutch simulates a launch assist system, helping players get faster starts off the line in supported games. Manual-gearbox fans can add the optional Logitech G Shifter.

Choosing the Right Version

The G923 is sold in two versions that aren’t interchangeable. The PlayStation version works with PS5, PS4 and PC, and the Xbox version works with Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One and PC. Buyers should choose the version that matches their console; PC players can use either. Wheel sensitivity and force feedback can be fine-tuned with Logitech G HUB software on PC.

Setup and Space

The wheel clamps to a desk or table, and the pedals sit on the floor, so the G923 needs a sturdy surface and a comfortable seat at the right height. A dedicated racing stand or cockpit, sold separately, gives a firmer setup, and the pedals’ M6 mount points allow bolting down. The wheel runs from mains power through its included adapter. Buyers should confirm it has a UK three-pin plug for Kenyan sockets, and a surge protector helps guard against spikes when power returns after an outage.
