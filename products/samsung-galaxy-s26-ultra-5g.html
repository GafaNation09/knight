Design and Build Quality

The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra refines the Ultra formula with a slimmer 7.9mm profile — down from the S25 Ultra’s 8.2mm — while maintaining the large 6.9-inch display and 5000mAh battery within a body that now weighs 214 grams, six grams lighter than its predecessor. The Armor Aluminum 2 frame is Samsung’s most rigid and lightweight aluminium alloy, contributing to both structural stiffness and the reduced weight.

The most significant build upgrade is Corning Gorilla Armor 2 on the front glass — the next generation of Samsung’s anti-reflective display glass that now achieves Mohs level 6 scratch resistance alongside the DX anti-reflective coating that reduces glare and reflections in outdoor use. The combination of scratch resistance and anti-glare properties makes the S26 Ultra’s front glass the most practical for outdoor daylight use of any Samsung flagship to date. The Privacy Display mode allows the screen content to be visible only from directly in front, blocking side-angle viewing for added privacy in public or office environments.

The back uses Gorilla Glass Victus 2 for strong scratch and drop resistance, and the S Pen is stored in its familiar silo at the bottom of the device. The six colour options — Cobalt Violet, Sky Blue, Black, White, Silver Shadow, and Pink Gold — are the most varied in Samsung’s Ultra lineup history, covering the full spectrum from understated professional to expressive and bold.

The IP68 waterproofing provides reliable protection against submersion up to 1.5 metres for 30 minutes, and the Class A drop rating covering 270 free-fall drops confirms Samsung’s commitment to durability in the Ultra lineup — a particularly meaningful credential for a flagship device used intensively throughout the working day.

Display Quality

The 6.9-inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X display at 1440 x 3120 pixels and 500 ppi is Samsung’s finest panel to date. The QHD+ resolution at 500 ppi renders text, images, and video with extraordinary sharpness that remains perceptible even at close viewing distances, and the 90.7 percent screen-to-body ratio creates an immersive, near-bezel-free visual experience across the large display area.

The 2600 nits peak brightness sits above the S25 Ultra’s rating, though independent testing measured 1480 nits in real-world conditions — a strong outdoor performance result that handles Kenya’s direct sunlight comfortably. The LTPO technology enables adaptive refresh between 1Hz and 120Hz, providing smooth gaming and scrolling while conserving battery during static content display. HDR10+ support delivers accurate, vivid colour reproduction across compatible streaming platforms.

Gorilla Armor 2 with DX anti-reflective coating is the feature that most differentiates the S26 Ultra’s display experience from other flagship phones in Kenya. The DX coating significantly reduces surface reflections compared to standard glass, making outdoor use more comfortable by reducing the white glare that washes out displays in bright sunlight — a practically important feature for outdoor use in Nairobi. The Mohs level 6 scratch resistance means everyday contact with keys, coins, and rough surfaces in a pocket or bag is handled without marking the display.

Camera Performance

Rear Camera System

The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra’s quad-camera system is the most capable available on any Android phone in Kenya. The 200MP primary camera upgrades its aperture to f/1.4 — the widest ever on a Samsung Ultra primary lens — and moves to a 1/1.3-inch sensor that is one of the largest available in smartphone photography. The wider aperture and larger sensor combine to capture more light per pixel than any previous Ultra primary camera, producing more detailed and cleaner results in low-light and indoor conditions.

Multi-directional PDAF and OIS on the primary camera ensure fast, accurate focus and stable capture across all shooting conditions. At full 200MP resolution, images contain extraordinary detail, while binned 12MP and 50MP output modes provide faster processing for everyday shooting.

The 50MP periscope telephoto with 5x optical zoom and OIS delivers the reliable, sharp zoom results the Ultra series is known for, while the 10MP 3x telephoto provides an intermediate zoom option for portrait-distance and mid-range subjects. The 50MP ultrawide with dual-pixel PDAF, f/1.9 aperture — the widest available on any Samsung ultrawide — and Super Steady video mode is the most capable ultrawide Samsung has put on any phone, capturing detailed wide-angle shots and providing high-quality stabilised video across all lighting conditions.

Galaxy AI camera features add meaningful creative and practical capability. Best Face selects the best facial expression from multiple captures in group shots. Horizon Lock keeps the horizon level during handheld video regardless of device tilt. Generative Edit removes, moves, or extends elements in photos. ProVisual Engine improves colour accuracy, sharpness, and noise reduction across all four lenses.

Video reaches 8K at 30fps and 4K at up to 120fps with 10-bit HDR and HDR10+ colour, gyro-EIS stabilisation, and stereo sound recording — making the S26 Ultra a comprehensive professional video tool for content creators in Kenya.

Selfie Camera

The 12MP front camera with f/2.2 aperture and dual-pixel PDAF records 4K video at up to 60fps with HDR10+ colour. It delivers sharp, well-exposed results for video calls, social media content, and selfie photography, with automatic HDR processing ensuring consistent output across challenging lighting conditions.

Performance and Software

The Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 on a 3nm process is the most powerful mobile processor ever produced. The two Oryon V3 Phoenix L performance cores running at 4.74GHz — the highest mobile CPU clock speed ever achieved — combined with six efficiency cores at 3.62GHz deliver an AnTuTu v11 score of 3,892,165, a GeekBench v6 score of 11,566, and a 3DMark Wild Life Extreme score of 7,744. These scores place the S26 Ultra ahead of every competing Android flagship and, for the first time, above Apple’s latest A-series chips in multiple benchmark categories.

The Adreno 840 GPU running at 1.3GHz is the most powerful mobile GPU ever measured, enabling the most graphically demanding games at maximum settings with sustained frame rates that no competing device has previously matched. For video professionals, the GPU accelerates 8K video processing, real-time HDR rendering, and ProRes-equivalent colour workflows that previously required desktop hardware.

With up to 16GB of LPDDR5X RAM and UFS 4.X storage — the fastest storage standard available — the S26 Ultra manages the most demanding workflows: multi-stream 8K video editing, AI image processing on large RAW files, multiple professional applications open simultaneously, and complex gaming without any compromise.

One UI 8.5 on Android 16 is Samsung’s most refined software release, and the seven major Android OS upgrade commitment ensures the S26 Ultra will receive updates until approximately 2033 — matching the longest software support available on any Android device globally. Galaxy AI features are deeply integrated throughout: Circle to Search identifies anything on screen by drawing around it, Live Translate handles real-time multilingual call translation, Note Assist summarises handwritten and typed notes, Chat Assist refines messages across any app, and Drawing Assist creates illustrations from sketch inputs.

Samsung DeX and Wireless DeX transform the S26 Ultra into a desktop computer when connected to a monitor via USB-C or wirelessly, running apps in resizable windows with keyboard and mouse support — a professional productivity capability unique to Samsung’s flagship lineup. Ultra Wideband enables precise spatial awareness for file sharing and Galaxy ecosystem device detection. Bluetooth 6.0 brings improved connection efficiency and Auracast broadcast audio support.

Battery Life and Charging

The 5000mAh battery delivers 16 hours 23 minutes of active use in independent testing and a 55-hour endurance rating — strong all-day and well-into-the-next-day performance for a flagship with a QHD+ display and the most powerful Android chip ever produced. The battery is rated for 1200 charge cycles, above the 1000-cycle standard found on most competitors, ensuring the cell retains strong capacity through several years of daily charging.

The 60W wired fast charging is the most significant battery upgrade over the S25 Ultra, nearly doubling the 45W charging speed of the previous generation. Reaching 75 percent in 30 minutes means a short charging session before leaving home or during a lunch break restores most of the day’s capacity quickly and conveniently. The 25W Qi2.2 wireless charging — the latest wireless charging standard — provides fast, compatible cable-free charging at the best wireless speed currently available. The 4.5W reverse wireless charging allows the S26 Ultra to wirelessly charge Galaxy Buds, a Galaxy Watch, or other Qi-compatible accessories directly from the phone.

Pros and Cons

The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra has exceptional and comprehensive strengths. It delivers the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 with the highest AnTuTu v11 score ever recorded at 3,892,165, a 200MP quad-camera system with f/1.4 aperture and 1/1.3-inch primary sensor, 8K video with 4K 120fps and 10-bit HDR, Gorilla Armor 2 with DX anti-reflective coating and Mohs level 6 scratch resistance, Privacy Display mode, a 6.9-inch QHD+ 120Hz AMOLED at 500 ppi, 60W wired charging at 75% in 30 minutes, 25W Qi2.2 wireless charging, 1200 cycle battery rated for 55 hours endurance, built-in S Pen, seven major Android OS upgrades, Galaxy AI throughout One UI 8.5, Samsung DeX and Wireless DeX, Bluetooth 6.0, Wi-Fi 7, NFC, UWB, USB-C 3.2, and a Class A 270-drop rating — at KES 124,000.

Its main limitations are the absence of a microSD card slot, no 3.5mm headphone jack, IP68 rating to 1.5 metres rather than the deeper 6-metre rating found on flagship iPhones, 25W wireless charging that is strong but trails the 50W wireless on the Honor 600 Pro, and a measured display brightness of 1480 nits that is lower than the Honor 600 Pro’s 1766 nits and the Poco F8 Pro’s measured results despite the higher 2600 nit peak specification.
