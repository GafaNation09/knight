Samsung announced the Galaxy F36 5G on March 11, 2025, and made it available from March 13, 2025. It runs Android 15 with One UI 7 and promises up to six major Android upgrades, making it one of the best long-term software investments available at this price in Kenya today.

The F36 5G combines a 6.7-inch Super AMOLED display, the MediaTek Dimensity 6300 6nm chipset, a 50MP main camera with autofocus, a 5000mAh battery with 25W wired charging, IP54 dust and splash resistance, NFC, and 5G connectivity into a slim 7.9mm package. Design and Build Quality

The Samsung Galaxy F36 5G has a slim and modern design that feels refined for its price. At 7.9mm thick and 191 grams, it is comfortable to hold and carry throughout the day. The glass front gives it a premium look, while the plastic frame and back keep the weight low.

Samsung offers three distinct color options including Bling Black, Vibing Blue, and Glam Green. Each finish adds personality, so buyers can choose between a bold statement or a more subtle everyday look. Furthermore, IP54 dust and splash resistance means the phone handles light rain and accidental splashes without concern.

The side-mounted fingerprint sensor sits naturally where the thumb rests, making unlocking fast and intuitive. Additionally, the 86.0% screen-to-body ratio gives the display a modern edge-to-edge appearance that maximizes the viewing area.

Display Quality

The 6.7-inch Super AMOLED display is one of the strongest features of the Galaxy F36 5G. AMOLED technology delivers deep blacks, vivid colors, and strong contrast that IPS LCD panels cannot match at any resolution. As a result, everyday content like social media, YouTube, and streaming services looks noticeably richer and more engaging.

The 1080 x 2340 pixel Full HD+ resolution at approximately 385 ppi produces sharp and detailed visuals. Text is crisp, images are clear, and video content looks vibrant across all brightness levels. Moreover, 800 nits of brightness ensures comfortable outdoor readability in Kenya’s bright ambient conditions.

The 90Hz refresh rate makes scrolling and navigation feel fluid and responsive. However, it does not reach the 120Hz found on higher-end Samsung models, which is an expected trade-off at this price point.

Camera Performance

Rear Cameras

The Galaxy F36 5G features a triple rear camera system led by a 50MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and autofocus. The wide aperture captures strong detail and handles everyday lighting conditions well. Furthermore, autofocus ensures sharp subject locking across varying distances, which improves the consistency of everyday shots.

The 5MP ultra-wide camera adds scene flexibility for landscapes, architecture, and group photos where fitting more into the frame matters. Additionally, the 2MP macro lens allows close-up detail photography for food, flowers, and small objects. LED flash, HDR processing, and panorama support complete the rear camera feature set.

However, video recording tops out at 1080p rather than 4K, which is a limitation for users who prioritize high-resolution video content creation.

Selfie Camera

The 13MP front camera with f/2.0 aperture records at 1080p and handles everyday selfies, WhatsApp calls, and social media content reliably. The wide aperture performs better in lower light than narrower front cameras at this price level.

Performance and Software

The MediaTek Dimensity 6300 built on a 6nm process delivers capable mid-range performance. Two Cortex-A76 performance cores at 2.4 GHz handle demanding tasks, while six efficiency cores manage everyday workloads without draining the battery unnecessarily. As a result, the phone handles social media, messaging, browsing, streaming, and light multitasking smoothly.

With RAM options of 4GB, 6GB, and 8GB, buyers can choose the configuration that suits their multitasking needs. Therefore, the 8GB variant is the better long-term investment for users who switch frequently between multiple apps. The 128GB internal storage covers most everyday needs, and the microSDXC slot allows expansion when more space is needed.

The phone runs Android 15 with Samsung’s One UI 7 interface and supports up to six major Android OS upgrades. Consequently, the Galaxy F36 5G is one of the best long-term software investments available in the mid-range segment in Kenya at KES 23,500. Six years of major updates means the phone stays current, secure, and capable well beyond the typical two to three-year upgrade cycle.

Battery Life and Charging

The 5000mAh battery delivers reliable all-day endurance for most everyday usage patterns. Whether the day involves calls, social media, YouTube, maps, or work apps, the battery holds up without needing a midday top-up for most users.

The 25W wired charging restores battery capacity efficiently during short breaks. As a result, users spend less time tethered to a wall outlet and more time using the phone. However, the F36 5G does not support wireless charging, which is an expected omission at this price point.

Connectivity

5G support makes the Galaxy F36 5G one of the most future-ready phones available in Kenya at KES 23,500. As Kenya’s 5G network infrastructure continues to expand, buyers who invest in a 5G device today will benefit from faster data speeds without needing to upgrade their phone again in the near future.

NFC support adds contactless payment capability on compatible networks, which is a practical addition for everyday use. Bluetooth 5.3 covers wireless audio and device connections efficiently, and the multi-constellation GPS system covering GPS, GALILEO, GLONASS, BDS, and QZSS provides accurate positioning across all environments. Moreover, dual-band Wi-Fi with Wi-Fi Direct handles reliable wireless internet across 2.4GHz and 5GHz networks.

Samsung Galaxy F36 5G vs Samsung Galaxy A26

The Galaxy F36 5G and Samsung Galaxy A26 compete closely in the mid-range segment in Kenya. Both offer Samsung’s One UI experience and long-term software support. Therefore, buyers who prioritize 5G future-proofing and a larger screen will find the F36 5G the stronger choice. For buyers who prefer a more compact device or prioritize other specific features, the A26 remains a strong Samsung alternative.
